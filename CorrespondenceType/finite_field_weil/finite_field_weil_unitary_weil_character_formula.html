Unitary Weil Character Formula over a Finite Field

Let $(F,i)$ be a $K/k$-unitary space over a finite field $k$ of order $q$, and put $n=\dim_K F$. For every $u \in U(F,i)$, $$ \operatorname{Tr} W^{F,i}(u) = (-1)^n(-q)^{N(F;u)}, \qquad N(F;u)=\dim_K \ker(u-1). $$
