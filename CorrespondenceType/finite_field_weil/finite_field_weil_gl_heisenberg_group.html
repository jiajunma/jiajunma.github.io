#

Heisenberg Group Attached to a Finite Vector Space

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over a finite field $k$, and let $V^\vee$ be its dual. The Heisenberg group $H(V)$ is the set of triples $(x,y,z)$ with $x \in V$, $y \in V^\vee$, and $z \in k$, with multiplication $$ (x,y,z)(x',y',z') = (x+x',y+y',z+z' + y(x')). $$ Equivalently, $H(V)$ is the group of upper-triangular matrices $$ \begin{pmatrix} 1 & y & z \\ 0 & 1 & x \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}. $$ It is a central extension $$ 0 \to k \to H(V) \to V \times V^\vee \to 0. $$
