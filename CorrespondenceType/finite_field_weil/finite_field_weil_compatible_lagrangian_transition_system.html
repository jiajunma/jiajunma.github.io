Compatible System of Schrödinger-Model Intertwiners over a Finite Field

This node records the consequence of Orthogonality and Trivialization of the Change-of-Lagrangian Cocycle: over a finite field of odd characteristic the Schrödinger models of the Weil representation, attached to the various Lagrangians, fit into a compatible system of intertwiners — a coherent family of identifications closed under composition. It is the finite-field model of the kind of compatible system one wants on the $p$-adic side, and it shows the finite-field part of any transition map carries no metaplectic obstruction.

The formal compatible system below uses the base-Lagrangian coboundary trivialization. Gérardin's original proof that the full finite-field symplectic Weil projective cocycle linearizes is the separate input Gérardin Linearization of the Finite Symplectic Weil Cocycle.

Setup (recalled)

Notation as in Orthogonality and Trivialization of the Change-of-Lagrangian Cocycle: $k$ is a finite field of odd order $q$ and characteristic $p$, $(E,j)$ a nondegenerate symplectic $k$-space with $i=j/2$, $H(E,j)$ its Heisenberg group, and $\xi\colon k\to R^{\times}$ a nontrivial character into a field $R$ with $\operatorname{char}R\neq p$. For a Lagrangian $L\subseteq E$, $\mathcal S_L$ is the Schrödinger model, and

$$ \mathcal F_{L,L'}\colon\mathcal S_{L'}\to\mathcal S_L,\qquad (\mathcal F_{L,L'}\varphi)(v)=\sum_{a\in L/(L\cap L')}\xi(i(a,v))\,\varphi(v+a) $$

is the canonical $H(E,j)$-intertwiner. By that node these satisfy the orthogonality $\mathcal F_{L,L'}\circ\mathcal F_{L',L}=q^{\,d_{L,L'}}\operatorname{id}$ ($d_{L,L'}=m-\dim_k(L\cap L')$, $\dim_k E=2m$), compose by a cocycle $\mathcal F_{L,L'}\circ\mathcal F_{L',L''}=\gamma(L,L',L'')\mathcal F_{L,L''}$, and — fixing any base Lagrangian $L_0$ — the cocycle is a coboundary $\gamma=\delta c$ with the explicit, division-free character sum

$$ c_{L,L'}=\gamma(L,L',L_0)=\!\!\sum_{\substack{a\in L'/(L'\cap L_0),\ b\in L/(L\cap L')\\ a+b\in L_0}}\!\!\xi\big(i(a,b)\big)\in R^{\times}. $$

Statement

Put $\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}:=c_{L,L'}^{-1}\,\mathcal F_{L,L'}$. Then $\{\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}\}$ is a compatible system of $H(E,j)$-isomorphisms between the Schrödinger models: for all Lagrangians $L,L',L''$,

$$ \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L}=\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal S_L},\qquad \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}\circ\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L',L''}=\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L''},\qquad \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L',L}=\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}^{-1}. $$

Moreover the system is unique up to relabeling: for any other compatible system \(T_{L,L'}\), there are scalars \(\nu_L\in R^\times\) such that

$$ T_{L,L'}=(\nu_{L'}/\nu_L)\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}. $$

Proof

By Orthogonality and Trivialization of the Change-of-Lagrangian Cocycle, $\gamma=\delta c$, i.e. $\gamma(L,L',L'')=c_{L,L'}c_{L',L''}c_{L,L''}^{-1}$, and $c_{L,L}=1$. Hence

$$ \begin{aligned} \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}\circ\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L',L''} &= c_{L,L'}^{-1}c_{L',L''}^{-1} (\mathcal F_{L,L'}\circ\mathcal F_{L',L''})\\ &= c_{L,L'}^{-1}c_{L',L''}^{-1} \gamma(L,L',L'')\mathcal F_{L,L''}\\ &= c_{L,L''}^{-1}\mathcal F_{L,L''}\\ &= \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L''}. \end{aligned} $$

In the third line we used the coboundary identity for \(\gamma\). Also \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L}=c_{L,L}^{-1}\mathcal F_{L,L} =\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal S_L}\), since \(c_{L,L}=1\) and \(\mathcal F_{L,L}\) is the identity.

Taking \(L''=L\) in the strict composition law gives

$$ \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}\circ\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L',L} = \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L} = \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal S_L}. $$

Interchanging \(L\) and \(L'\) gives

$$ \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L',L}\circ\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'} = \operatorname{id}_{\mathcal S_{L'}}. $$

Thus \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L',L}=\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}^{-1}\). Each \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}\) is a nonzero scalar multiple of the \(H(E,j)\)-homomorphism \(\mathcal F_{L,L'}\), hence is itself an \(H(E,j)\)-isomorphism.

For uniqueness, let \(T_{L,L'}\) be any other compatible system built from nonzero \(H(E,j)\)-intertwiners between the same Schrödinger models. By One-Dimensional Hom Line for Finite Schrödinger Models, there are unique scalars \(\mu_{L,L'}\in R^\times\) such that

$$ T_{L,L'}=\mu_{L,L'}\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}. $$

Compatibility of \(T\) and of \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}\) means

$$ T_{L,L'}T_{L',L''}=T_{L,L''}, \qquad \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L',L''} = \widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L''}. $$

Substituting \(T_{A,B}=\mu_{A,B}\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{A,B}\) gives

$$ \mu_{L,L'}\mu_{L',L''}\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L''} = \mu_{L,L''}\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L''}. $$

Since \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L''}\neq0\), the coefficients are equal:

$$ \mu_{L,L'}\mu_{L',L''}=\mu_{L,L''},\qquad \mu_{L,L}=1. $$

Fix one base Lagrangian \(B\) and set \(\nu_L:=\mu_{B,L}\). Taking \((B,L,L')\) in the preceding identity gives

$$ \nu_L\mu_{L,L'}=\nu_{L'}, $$

so

$$ \mu_{L,L'}=\nu_L^{-1}\nu_{L'}. $$

Thus every compatible system differs from \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{L,L'}\) by rescaling the object \(\mathcal S_L\) by \(\nu_L^{-1}\), equivalently by the displayed factor \(\nu_{L'}/\nu_L\) on the map from \(\mathcal S_{L'}\) to \(\mathcal S_L\). This is the asserted relabeling. \(\square\)

Role of odd characteristic

Odd characteristic is essential in these nodes because it makes \(i=j/2\) available in the Heisenberg cocycle and in the covariance formulas. The condition \(\operatorname{char}R\neq p\) makes \(q\) a unit in \(R\), so the factor \(q^{d_{L,L'}}\) in the orthogonality identity is invertible. These are the only hypotheses needed for the compatible transition system proved above.

Interpretation for Lattice-Model Transitions

The result proved here is only the finite-field statement: the Lagrangian Schrödinger transition cocycle is a coboundary after choosing a base Lagrangian. Thus, when this finite calculation appears as a residual factor inside a \(p\)-adic lattice-model comparison, this finite Lagrangian cocycle is not by itself an obstruction to strict transition maps. Any remaining \(p\)-adic metaplectic splitting issue is a separate input, not proved in this finite-field node.
