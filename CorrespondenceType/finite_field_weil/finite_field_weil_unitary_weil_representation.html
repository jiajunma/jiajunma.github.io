Unitary Weil Representation over a Finite Field

Let $(F,i)$ be a $K/k$-unitary space of dimension $n$, and let $H(F,i)$ be its Heisenberg group. The unitary group $U(F,i)$ fixes the Stone-von Neumann class of each nontrivial central character. There is a distinguished representation class $$ W^{F,i} $$ of $U(F,i)$, called the unitary Weil representation, characterized by the property that the associated Heisenberg representation extends to $U(F,i)H(F,i)$ and restricts to $W^{F,i}$ on $U(F,i)$.

When $q$ is odd, under the isomorphism $H(E,j)\simeq H(F,i)$, this class satisfies $$ W^{F,i}=\chi_{F,i}\otimes W_\xi^{E,j}\big|_{U(F,i)}, $$ where $\chi_{F,i}$ is the unique nontrivial real one-dimensional representation of $U(F,i)$.

For $\dim_K F=1$, the class $W^{F,i}$ is the sum of all nontrivial one-dimensional representation classes of $U(F,i)$.
