General Linear Weil Representation over a Finite Field

Let $G(V)$ be the subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}(V \times V^\vee)$ consisting of pairs $(g,{}^\vee g)$, where ${}^\vee g$ is the contragredient transformation on $V^\vee$. The group $G(V)$ acts on $H(V)$ by automorphisms and fixes the center pointwise.

There is a unique representation class $W^V$ of $G(V)$, called the Weil representation of $G(V)$, such that for every irreducible representation $\eta$ of $H(V)$ nontrivial on the center, $\eta$ extends to a representation of the semidirect product $G(V)H(V)$ whose restriction to $G(V)$ lies in $W^V$, and the character of $W^V$ takes positive values.

In the Schrödinger realization on $\mathbf{C}[V]$, the action of $G(V) \simeq \operatorname{GL}(V)$ is $$ (gF)(x)=F(g^{-1}x). $$
