#

Finite-Field Unitary Space in Gérardin's Convention

Let $K/k$ be a quadratic extension of finite fields, with conjugation $a \mapsto \bar a$. A $K/k$-unitary space is a finite-dimensional $K$-vector space $F$ equipped with a nondegenerate skew-hermitian form $i$.

The underlying $k$-vector space $E$ carries the nondegenerate symplectic form $$ j(w,w')=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/k} i(w,w') =i(w,w')-i(w',w). $$ This gives an embedding $$ U(F,i)\hookrightarrow S(E,j). $$
