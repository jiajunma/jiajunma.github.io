Equivalence of Epipelagic Data

For classical groups appearing in type I dual pairs, two epipelagic representations \[ \pi_x(\lambda,\chi),\qquad \pi_y(\lambda',\chi') \] are isomorphic exactly when their data are conjugate in the expected sense: the building points and functionals are carried to one another by \(G\), and the stabilizer characters match under the induced stabilizer isomorphism.

Proof

The proof reduces isomorphism of compactly induced epipelagic representations to conjugacy of their unrefined minimal \(K\)-types. A lift of a stable functional is shown to be a good regular semisimple element, so its centralizer has building reduced to the relevant point. Kim-Murnaghan conjugacy of minimal \(K\)-types then forces the building points and stable functionals to match, and Proposition 2.2.1 compares the residual stabilizer characters.
