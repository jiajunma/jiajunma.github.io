Apartment Model for Sp(W) via Self-Dual Lattice Functions

Write \(W=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n H_i\) with hyperbolic planes \(H_i=Fe_i\oplus Ff_i\), \(\langle e_i,f_i\rangle=1\), so \(\dim_F W=2n\) and \(G=\operatorname{Sp}(W)\). A point of the standard apartment is \(\underline a=(a_1,\dots,a_n)\in\mathbb R^n\), giving the self-dual lattice function (conductor-\(\mathfrak p\)) whose value at level \(s\) is \[ \mathscr L_{\underline a,s}=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n\Big(\mathfrak p^{\lceil s-a_i\rceil}e_i\ \oplus\ \mathfrak p^{\lceil s+a_i\rceil}f_i\Big). \] This is self-dual in the sense of Self-Dual Lattice Function: \(\mathscr L_{\underline a,s}^{\#}=\mathscr L_{\underline a,(-s)+}\). It is the Broussous-Stevens MM-norm \(\alpha_p\) (Prop 4.1) read as a lattice function via \(\mathscr L_{\underline a,r}=\{w:\alpha_p(w)\ge r\}\); the sign of \(a_i\) is a Weyl convention, fixed here so that the \(e_i\)-exponent is \(\lceil s-a_i\rceil\) (the literal BS norm gives \(\lceil s+a_i\rceil\), i.e. \(a_i\mapsto-a_i\)). The affine Weyl group \(\widetilde C_n\) (signed permutations of the \(a_i\) together with integral translations) acts, and a fundamental alcove is \[ 0\le a_1\le a_2\le\cdots\le a_n\le\tfrac12 . \] By Broussous-Stevens, \(\underline a\mapsto\mathscr L_{\underline a}\) is the apartment chart of the bijection between points of \(\mathcal B(\operatorname{Sp}(W))\) and self-dual lattice functions. The lattice function is attached to the point \(\underline a\), not to its facet: within an open facet the lattice chain (the set of lattices) and the parahoric are fixed, but \(\mathscr L_{\underline a}\) still varies as the jump positions \(\pm a_i\) move.

Jump dictionary

Plane \(H_i\) jumps at \(s\equiv a_i\) (the \(e_i\)-line) and \(s\equiv -a_i\) (the \(f_i\)-line). Hence:

  • \(a_i=0\): \(\bar e_i,\bar f_i\) lie in \(\operatorname{gr}_0\) — the first symplectic end.
  • \(a_i=\tfrac12\): \(\bar e_i,\bar f_i\) lie in \(\operatorname{gr}_{1/2}\) — the second symplectic end.
  • \(a_i\in(0,\tfrac12)\): a \(\operatorname{GL}_1\) pair \((\operatorname{gr}_{a_i},\operatorname{gr}_{-a_i})\).

Equal coordinates merge their blocks: a cluster of \(p\) zeros gives \(\operatorname{Sp}_{2p}\), a cluster of \(r\) halves gives \(\operatorname{Sp}_{2r}\), and a cluster of \(k\) equal interior values gives \(\operatorname{GL}_k\). This reproduces the reductive quotient of Parahoric Reductive Quotient for Sp(W): \[ \mathsf G_{\underline a}=\operatorname{Sp}_{2p}\times\operatorname{Sp}_{2r}\times\prod_c\operatorname{GL}_{k_c}, \qquad p+r+\sum_c k_c=n . \]

A facet is a coincidence pattern among \(\{0,a_1,\dots,a_n,\tfrac12\}\); a face is obtained by imposing strictly more coincidences (moving coordinates to \(0\) or \(\tfrac12\), or merging clusters).

Example: Sp(4)

Alcove \(0\le a_1\le a_2\le\tfrac12\):

  • \(v_0=(0,0)\): \(\mathsf G=\operatorname{Sp}_4\), with \(\operatorname{gr}_0\) of dimension \(4\).
  • \(v_1=(0,\tfrac12)\): \(\mathsf G=\operatorname{Sp}_2\times\operatorname{Sp}_2\) — both ends nonzero, \(\operatorname{gr}_0\) and \(\operatorname{gr}_{1/2}\) each of dimension \(2\). This vertex has no analogue for \(\operatorname{Sp}_2\).
  • \(v_2=(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)\): \(\mathsf G=\operatorname{Sp}_4\) at the second end.
  • chamber \(0<a_1<a_2<\tfrac12\): \(\mathsf G=\operatorname{GL}_1\times\operatorname{GL}_1\) (Iwahori).

The level-\(0\) lattice depends only on which coordinates vanish: \(\mathscr L_{\underline a,0}=\bigoplus_i(\mathfrak o e_i\oplus\mathfrak p^{\,[a_i>0]}f_i)\). The incidence of a point of the edge \((0,a_2)\), \(0<a_2<\tfrac12\), over \(v_0\), and the resulting canonical isotropic flag on \(\operatorname{gr}_0\mathscr L_{v_0}\), are worked out in Facet Incidence as a Parabolic for Sp(W).
