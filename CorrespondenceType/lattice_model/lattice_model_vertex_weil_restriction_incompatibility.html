Vertex Weil Restrictions over an Alcove Are Incompatible

Fix an alcove \(C\) of \(\mathcal B(\operatorname{Sp}(W))\), \(\dim_F W=2n\). The pro-unipotent fixed spaces \(\omega_\psi^{\,G_{L_i,0^+}}\cong S(\mathsf b_{L_i})\) at the vertices \(L_0,\dots,L_n\) of \(C\) (Pro-Unipotent Fixed Vectors of the Weil Representation at a Good Lattice) carry the finite Weil representation of the level-\(0\) factor of each parahoric reductive quotient. This node shows that these per-vertex trivializations cannot be glued into a single \(G_C\)-equivariant datum (a coefficient system) on \(C\), and — sharper — that the obstruction cannot be removed by any twist by characters of the reductive quotients: it is a genuine nontrivial quadratic (Weil-index) character of the Iwahori torus.

Setup

Let \(F,\mathfrak o,\mathfrak p,k=\mathfrak o/\mathfrak p\) (odd \(q\)), \(\varpi\), \(\psi\) of conductor \(\mathfrak p\), \((W,\langle\,,\rangle)\) symplectic of dimension \(2n\), \(G=\operatorname{Sp}(W)\), \(\omega_\psi\) the Weil representation. The building is of type \(\widetilde C_n\), so the alcove \(C\) has \(n+1\) vertices \(L_0,\dots,L_n\), indexed (in the apartment chart of Apartment Model for Sp(W) via Self-Dual Lattice Functions) so that

$$ \dim_k\big(L_i/L_i^{\#}\big)=2i,\qquad L_i^{\#}=\{w\in W:\langle w,L_i\rangle\subseteq\mathfrak p\}. $$

Thus \(\mathsf b_{L_i}=L_i/L_i^{\#}\cong k^{2i}\); the extreme vertices are \(L_0\) (\(\#\)-self-dual, \(\mathsf b_{L_0}=0\)) and \(L_n\) (\(L_n^{\#}=\mathfrak p L_n\), \(\mathsf b_{L_n}\cong k^{2n}\)). Let \(G_C=G_{C,0}\) be the Iwahori (stabilizer of \(C\)), \(G_{C,0^+}\) its pro-unipotent radical, and

$$ T:=G_C/G_{C,0^+}\cong(k^\times)^n $$

the Iwahori torus, with coordinates \(t=(t_1,\dots,t_n)\).

Because \(G_C\subseteq G_{L_i,0}\), the image of \(G_C\) in \(\mathsf G_{L_i}\) is a Borel subgroup with unipotent radical \(\operatorname{im}(G_{C,0^+})\) and Levi \(T\) (Facet Incidence as a Parabolic for Sp(W)); and \(G_{L_i,0^+}\subseteq G_{C,0^+}\), so the common Iwahori stalk

$$ \ell:=\omega_\psi^{\,G_{C,0^+}}=S(\mathsf b_{L_i})^{\,\operatorname{im}(G_{C,0^+})} $$

sits inside every \(S(\mathsf b_{L_i})\). It is one-dimensional (the maximal unipotent of \(\operatorname{Sp}(\mathsf b_{L_i})\) fixes only the Schrödinger line \(\mathbb C\delta_0\)), and \(T\) acts on it by a character. For each \(i\) write \(\chi^{(i)}\colon T\to\mathbb C^\times\) for the character through which \(T\) acts on \(\ell\) via the vertex-\(L_i\) trivialization Pro-Unipotent Fixed Vectors of the Weil Representation at a Good Lattice. Call the trivializations compatible if all \(\chi^{(i)}\) coincide (the condition for them to glue to a \(G_C\)-equivariant datum on \(C\)). A twist is a family of characters \(\mu_i\colon\mathsf G_{L_i}=\operatorname{Sp}(\mathsf b_{L_i})\times\operatorname{Sp}(L_i^{\#}/\mathfrak p L_i)\to\mathbb C^\times\), changing \(\chi^{(i)}\) to \(\chi^{(i)}\cdot(\mu_i|_T)\); it matches the vertices if the twisted characters all coincide.

Statement

Let \(\chi_{\mathrm{quad}}\colon k^\times\to\{\pm1\}\) be the quadratic character. Then, in coordinates so that the level-\(0\) planes of \(L_n\) are all \(n\) coordinates,

$$ \chi^{(0)}=\mathbf 1,\qquad \chi^{(n)}(t)=\prod_{j=1}^{n}\chi_{\mathrm{quad}}(t_j), $$

and no twist matches the vertices. In particular the per-vertex trivializations are incompatible, and the discrepancy

$$ \delta:=\chi^{(n)}\,(\chi^{(0)})^{-1}=\prod_{j=1}^{n}\chi_{\mathrm{quad}}(t_j) $$

is a nontrivial character of \(T\) of order \(2\) that is not the restriction of any character of the reductive quotients; the incompatibility is intrinsic.

Proof

It suffices to compare the two extreme vertices \(L_0,L_n\).

Computation of \(\chi^{(0)}\) and \(\chi^{(n)}\). At \(L_0\) one has \(\mathsf b_{L_0}=0\), so \(S(\mathsf b_{L_0})=\mathbb C\) and, by Pro-Unipotent Fixed Vectors of the Weil Representation at a Good Lattice, the whole reductive quotient — hence \(T\) — acts trivially: \(\chi^{(0)}=\mathbf 1\). At \(L_n\) the level-\(0\) factor is \(\operatorname{Sp}_{2n}(k)\) and the image of \(T\) is its split maximal torus \(\operatorname{diag}(t_1,\dots,t_n,t_1^{-1},\dots,t_n^{-1})\); on the Schrödinger unipotent-fixed line \(\mathbb C\delta_0\) the finite Weil representation (Symplectic Weil Representation over a Finite Field) acts by \(t\mapsto\prod_{j}\chi_{\mathrm{quad}}(t_j)\), so \(\chi^{(n)}(t)=\prod_{j=1}^n\chi_{\mathrm{quad}}(t_j)\). As \(\chi_{\mathrm{quad}}\) is nontrivial (the squares have index \(2\) in \(k^\times\) for \(q\) odd), \(\delta\) is a nontrivial character of order \(2\).

No twist matches. Each reductive quotient \(\mathsf G_{L_i}=\operatorname{Sp}(\mathsf b_{L_i})\times\operatorname{Sp}(L_i^{\#}/\mathfrak p L_i)\) is a product of finite symplectic groups (Parahoric Reductive Quotient for Sp(W)). Each finite symplectic group \(\operatorname{Sp}_{2m}(k)\) is perfect, with the single exception \(\operatorname{Sp}_2(\mathbb F_3)\) whose abelianization is cyclic of order \(3\); hence every character of \(\operatorname{Sp}_{2m}(k)\) has odd order, and so does every character of \(\mathsf G_{L_i}\) and its restriction \(\mu_i|_T\). Suppose a twist \(\{\mu_i\}\) matched the vertices. Comparing at \(L_0\) and \(L_n\),

$$ \chi^{(0)}\cdot(\mu_0|_T)=\chi^{(n)}\cdot(\mu_1|_T)\ \Longrightarrow\ \delta=(\mu_0|_T)(\mu_n|_T)^{-1}. $$

The right-hand side has odd order (a product of odd-order characters), while \(\delta\) has order \(2\) — impossible. Hence no twist matches the vertices; in particular the untwisted trivializations (\(\mu_i=\mathbf 1\)) are already incompatible since \(\chi^{(0)}\neq\chi^{(n)}\). \(\square\)

Remarks

  • Intrinsic obstruction. The discrepancy is the nontrivial quadratic (Weil-index) character \(\prod_j\chi_{\mathrm{quad}}(t_j)\) of \(T\). It records the difference of the lattice-model splittings \(\xi_{L_i}|_{G_C}\) between vertices: the level-\(\tfrac12\) factor is "trivial" only in each vertex's own gauge, and gluing into the ambient \(\omega_\psi\) restores a \(\chi_{\mathrm{quad}}\) per level-\(\tfrac12\) plane. Because the reductive quotients are perfect, this sign lives in no reductive-quotient character and cannot be twisted away.

  • Consequence for building localization. The naive assignment \(L_i\mapsto\omega_\psi^{G_{L_i,0^+}}\cong S(\mathsf b_{L_i})\) does not define a \(G\)-equivariant coefficient system on \(\mathcal B(\operatorname{Sp}(W))\) by the level-\(0\) finite Weil identifications alone; a Weil-index correction is forced.
