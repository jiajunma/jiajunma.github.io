Single Positive Depth Theta Lift of Data

Let \(\Sigma=(x,\Gamma,\phi,\rho)\) be a single positive-depth block of depth \(r=2s\) for \(G=U(V)\). The target hermitian space is the isometry class of \(V_\Gamma\). Choose \(w\in V\otimes_D V_\Gamma\) such that \[ M(w)=\Gamma. \] Define the target lattice function by \[ L'_t=wL_{t+s}, \] and let \(x'\) be the corresponding building point. Set \[ \Gamma'=M'(w). \] Conjugation by \(w\) gives an isomorphism between the relevant centralizers, and the target character and cuspidal finite quotient representation are the transported contragredients: \[ \phi'=\phi^\ast ∘ \alpha^{-1},\qquad \rho'=\rho^\ast ∘ \alpha^{-1}. \] The resulting datum \[ \Sigma'=(x',\Gamma',\phi',\rho') \] is well-defined up to equivalence, independent of the representative of \([\Sigma]\) and of the choice of \(w\). Its equivalence class is denoted \(\#^+([\Sigma])\).
