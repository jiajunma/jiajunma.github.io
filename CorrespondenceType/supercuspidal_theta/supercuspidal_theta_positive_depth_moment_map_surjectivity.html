Positive Depth Moment Map Surjectivity

Let \(\Sigma=(x,\Gamma,\phi,\rho)\) be a single positive-depth block of depth \(r=2s\). Let \(L\) be the lattice function attached to \(x\), let \(L'\) be a self-dual lattice function in a target space \(V'\), set \[ B=L\otimes_D L', \] and choose \(w\in B_s\) such that \[ M(w)\in \Gamma+\mathbf g_{x,r+}. \] Then for every \(t>-s\), \[ M(w+B_t)=M(w)+\mathbf g_{x,s+t}. \]

This is the local geometric input behind the positive-depth block lift: it says that once a moment-map point has the right leading term, all deeper perturbations in the Moy-Prasad lattice are reached by perturbing \(w\) inside the oscillator lattice model.

Proof

The inclusion \[ M(w+B_t)\subseteq M(w)+\mathbf g_{x,s+t} \] is the direct lattice calculation: if \(b\in B_t\), then \(wb^\ast+bw^\ast+bb^\ast\) has Moy-Prasad depth at least \(s+t\).

For the opposite inclusion, fix an element \(Z\in M(w)+\mathbf g_{x,s+t}\). The key infinitesimal claim is that the linearized moment-map operator on the graded piece \[ B_t/B_{t+}\to \mathbf g_{x,s+t}/\mathbf g_{x,s+t+} \] is surjective. The paper proves this by writing the linearization as \[ X\mapsto \Gamma_LX+X^\ast\Gamma_L. \] On the orthogonal complement of the centralizer of \(\Gamma\), this is the commutator map with \(\Gamma\), which is an isomorphism on the relevant graded quotient by the good-element property. On the centralizer part, the same map is multiplication by \(2\Gamma_L\) after taking the correct involution eigenspace; the large-residue-characteristic hypotheses make this invertible.

Starting with \(w_1=w\) and \(t_1=t\), use the surjectivity claim to choose \(b_i\in B_{t_i}\) so that \[ M(w_i+b_i)\in Z+\mathbf g_{x,s+t_i+}. \] Let \(w_{i+1}=w_i+b_i\), and let \(t_{i+1}\) be the next jump of the Moy-Prasad lattice after \(t_i\). Since the jump set is discrete and \(t_i\to\infty\), the sequence \(w_i\) converges to some \(w_\infty\in w+B_t\). Continuity of the moment map gives \(M(w_\infty)=Z\). This proves the required equality.
