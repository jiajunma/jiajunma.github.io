Fintzen 2021 — correction to Yu's construction

Historical context

Yu's 2001 paper Construction of tame supercuspidal representations gave an iterated Heisenberg–Weil construction of supercuspidal representations for tame reductive $p$-adic groups. The construction depended on:

  • a sequence of generic characters $\vec\phi = (\phi_0, \dots, \phi_d)$;
  • repeated application of Heisenberg–Weil twists at each stratum;
  • verification that the resulting $K_x$-representation is irreducible and satisfies the relevant intertwiner identities.

The gap. In the proof of the intertwiner identities (Yu §§12–14, especially Prop 14.1 and the proof of Thm 14.2), Yu's argument used an implicit assumption about the compatibility of the Heisenberg–Weil twists across the twisted Levi tower that was not justified in full generality. Specifically, the proof needed certain sign characters to vanish or to factor through a specific quotient — a fact that turned out to require a genuine additional hypothesis on the representation.

Fintzen's fix (2021)

[Fintzen 2021] gives a complete corrected proof of the irreducibility and supercuspidality of Yu's construction by:

  1. Identifying the precise hypothesis missing in Yu's argument (now made explicit; satisfied for $p$ large enough relative to the root system).
  2. Re-deriving the affected intertwiner calculations using a cleaner approach via the Heisenberg–Weil representations of the residue-field reductive quotient.
  3. Establishing that, under the explicit hypothesis on $p$, all the intertwiner identities (corresponding to Yu Prop 14.1, Thm 14.2, and the associated Yu §12–13 lemmas) hold in their original form.

The output: Yu's Theorem 14.2 (the main construction result) is true under the corrected hypothesis, and the proof in Fintzen 2021 is the standard reference.

Implication for citing "Yu" in this KB

In the original Yu 2001 numbering, the lemmas we depend on are:

  • Yu Thm 9.4 (two-term intertwiner)
  • Yu Lem 12.4, 12.5 (Hom dimension via index counts)
  • Yu Lem 13.6, 13.7 (index identities + intersection factorization)
  • Yu Prop 14.1, Thm 14.2 (one-dimensionality + supercuspidality)

After Fintzen 2021:

  • The statements of these lemmas remain correct.
  • The proofs — in particular the proofs of Prop 14.1 / Thm 14.2 — should cite Fintzen 2021 (which redoes them correctly), not Yu 2001 directly.
  • Dat–Fintzen 2026 reflects the corrected version: when DF cites a "Yu" lemma, they implicitly use the Fintzen-corrected proof.

Convention adopted in this KB

For tame-side proofs in Hom(κ_x, κ_y)_{K∩K} one-dim and G^{(0)}-trivial (proves (E3)) and Two-term intertwiner I(φ_y, φ_x) = G_{x,s} G' G_{y,s} (Yu Thm 9.4 generalized) (and any downstream node that depends on Yu §§12–14):

  • Primary source: [Dat–Fintzen 2026] — uses the corrected lemmas via the wild-inertia idempotent framework.
  • Secondary source: [Fintzen 2021] — explicit corrected proofs of the affected Yu lemmas.
  • Tertiary / historical: [Yu 2001] — the original statements (correct) with the original proofs (Prop 14.1 / Thm 14.2 incomplete).

The blueprint should cite DF + Fintzen 2021 as the authoritative source for intertwiner calculations, with Yu 2001 listed only for the historical notation alignment.
