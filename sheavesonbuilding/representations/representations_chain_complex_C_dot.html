Schneider–Stuhler chain complex $C_\bullet(\mathscr{V})$

For $\mathscr{V} \in \Cosh_G(X, R)$, define the chain complex $$C_d(\mathscr{V}) \;:=\; \bigoplus_{E \in [\![X]\!]_d} \mathscr{V}_E,$$ with boundary maps induced by the cosheaf restrictions $r^\mathscr{V}_{E', E}$ for $E' \preceq E$ (with signs from a chosen orientation of cells).

The $G$-action on $\mathscr{V}$ makes each $C_d(\mathscr{V})$ a smooth $R[G]$-module, and $\partial : C_d \to C_{d-1}$ is $G$-equivariant.

If $\mathscr{V} \in \Cosh_G(X, R, \phi)$, then $C_\bullet(\mathscr{V})$ is a chain complex in $\Rep_\phi(G)$ (see The subcategory Rep_φ(G)).

The functor $\mathscr{V} \mapsto H_0(C_\bullet(\mathscr{V}))$ from $\Cosh_G(X, R, \phi)$ to $\Rep_\phi(G)$ is the left adjoint of $\Loc_\phi$ in [[node:representations.h0_C_dot_adjoint]].

Augmentation

For a vertex $x \in [\![X]\!]_0$ and $V \in \Rep_\phi(G)$, applying this construction to $\mathscr{V} = \Loc_\phi(V)$ gives a chain complex with canonical augmentation $$\epsilon_V : C_0(\Loc_\phi(V)) = \bigoplus_x V[\phi_x] \to V, \qquad \epsilon_V(\textstyle\sum_x x \otimes v_x) = \textstyle\sum_x v_x.$$ $\epsilon_V$ kills boundaries and factors through $H_0$; this gives the counit of the adjunction.
