Per-case verifications of $K_x$-local (A1)

This node collects proofs that the $K_x$-local form of the apartment-extension axiom holds in each of the three settings used in the paper:

  • (a) Parahoric case ($G = G'$, $K_x = G_x$);
  • (b) Classical case ([Stevens] semisimple types, $p \neq 2$);
  • (c) Tame case (Kim–Yu–AFMO construction, $p$ large).

The $K_x$-local (A1) hypothesis is the one consumed by the §7.2 cartesian unit-iso assembly (Unit-iso assembly: V_F ≅ e_F H_0 via surjectivity + injectivity): under the equivalent algebraic form $$\hat K_x \;\subseteq\; N \cdot \hat K_{E_1} \cdot \hat K_{E_2} \cdot N \qquad \text{for all cells } E_1, E_2 \in [\![A]\!],$$ ([[node:building.A1_axiom_in_component]] Step 2 in the $K_x$-local form), what we need is: every element of $\hat K_x$ admits a decomposition $h = n_1 h_1 h_2 n_2$ with $n_i \in N$, $h_i \in \hat K_{E_i}$.

(a) Parahoric case $K_x = G_x$, $\hat K_x = \Stab_G(x)$

Lemma (parahoric A1). When $G = G'$ and $K_x = G_x$ is the parahoric subgroup at $x$, $K_x$-local (A1) holds. Equivalently $\hat K_x = G_x \cdot \Stab_N(x) \subseteq N \cdot G_{E_1} \cdot G_{E_2} \cdot N$ for any cells $E_1, E_2 \in [\![A]\!]$.

Proof

Decompose $\hat K_x = G_x \cdot \Stab_N(x)$, the fixator-coset decomposition for $\Fix_G(x)$ in the parahoric setting (Sheaf7 \texttt{lem:Stab_gx}).

Step 1: $G_x \subseteq N \cdot G_{E_1} \cdot G_{E_2} \cdot N$.

Classical Bruhat–Tits (A1) for $\mathcal B(G)$ says: any two cells of $\mathcal B(G)$ contained in a common apartment satisfy an Iwahori-Bruhat decomposition. Concretely, for our $E_1, E_2 \subseteq A$: $$G \;=\; G_{E_1} \cdot \widetilde N \cdot G_{E_2}$$ where $\widetilde N$ is the affine-Weyl normaliser of the maximal $F$-split torus $S$ corresponding to $A$. In our notation $\widetilde N = N$ (the normaliser-of-$S$-in-$G$, with the apartment structure inherited). Since $G_x \subseteq G$: $$G_x \;\subseteq\; G \;=\; G_{E_1} \cdot N \cdot G_{E_2} \;\subseteq\; N \cdot G_{E_1} \cdot G_{E_2} \cdot N$$ (by absorbing $N$-conjugation into the parahorics, using that $N$ normalises each parahoric in its affine Weyl class).

Step 2: $\Stab_N(x) \subseteq N$.

Trivial: $\Stab_N(x) \subseteq N \subseteq N \cdot \{e\} \cdot \{e\} \cdot N$, taking $h_1 = h_2 = e$.

Combining: $\hat K_x = G_x \cdot \Stab_N(x) \subseteq (N G_{E_1} G_{E_2} N) \cdot N \subseteq N G_{E_1} G_{E_2} N$ (closure of $N$ under multiplication on the right).

In the parahoric case $G_{E_i} = \hat K_{E_i}$, so this is the claimed inclusion. $\square$

Corollary (parahoric $C_0 = K_x \cdot A$). In the parahoric case, $C_0 = K_x \cdot A = G_x \cdot A$ is the entire extended Bruhat–Tits building $\mathcal B(G)$ — i.e.\ $\BSigma = \mathcal B(G)$ is connected and $K_x \cdot A = \BSigma$.

(b) Classical case (Stevens semisimple types, $p \neq 2$)

Lemma (classical A1). Let $G$ be a classical group ($p \neq 2$), $\beta$ a semisimple element, $G' := G^\beta$ the centraliser, and $K_x = J^\circ(\beta, \Lambda_x)$ the Stevens group at $x$ relative to a semisimple lattice sequence $\Lambda_x$ ([[node:applications.classical_K_F_definition]]). Then $K_x$-local (A1) holds.

Reduction to the enveloping general linear group. Let $\widetilde G := \mathrm{GL}_F(V)$, $\widetilde G^{(i)} := \widetilde G^{\gamma_i}$ along the decomposition $\beta = \sum_i \gamma_i e_i$ of the semisimple stratum ([[node:applications.classical_K1_K2_K3]]). Then $G \hookrightarrow \widetilde G$ is a closed embedding, the Bruhat–Tits building $\mathcal B(G)$ embeds (after a choice of maximal split torus) into $\mathcal B(\widetilde G)$, and the Stevens groups arise as $$K_x \;=\; \widetilde K_x \cap G, \qquad \hat K_x \;=\; \mathrm{Stab}_{\widetilde G}(x) \cap G,$$ where $\widetilde K_x = J^\circ(\beta, \Lambda_x)_{\widetilde G}$ is the analogous lattice-function group inside $\widetilde G$ (see [Stevens:SC §3.4], [Stevens:SS §3.5–§3.6]).

Step 1: $\widetilde G$ satisfies (A1) globally.

Since $\widetilde G = \mathrm{GL}_F(V)$, its extended Bruhat–Tits building $\mathcal B(\widetilde G)$ has the classical (A1) axiom in full generality (any two cells lie in a common apartment). Equivalently $\widetilde G = \widetilde G_{E_1} \cdot \widetilde N \cdot \widetilde G_{E_2}$ where $\widetilde N$ is the $\widetilde G$-normaliser of a maximal $F$-split torus.

Step 2: descent to $\hat K_x$.

By the Glauberman fixed-point construction ([Stevens:SS §3.6], cf.\ [[node:applications.classical_K1_K2_K3]]), $$\hat K_x \;=\; \widetilde{\mathfrak j}_x \cap G$$ where $\widetilde{\mathfrak j}_x = \sum_i \widetilde{\mathfrak g}^{(i)}_{x, s_i}$ is the sum of Moy–Prasad pieces in the $\widetilde G^{(i)}$'s.

For $\widetilde h \in \hat K_x \subseteq \widetilde G$, apply $\widetilde G$-Iwahori–Bruhat: $\widetilde h \in \widetilde G_{E_1} \cdot \widetilde N \cdot \widetilde G_{E_2}$. Write $\widetilde h = \widetilde h_1 \widetilde n \widetilde h_2$ with $\widetilde h_i \in \widetilde G_{E_i}$, $\widetilde n \in \widetilde N$.

Step 3: pieces descend to $G$.

The decomposition $\widetilde h = \widetilde h_1 \widetilde n \widetilde h_2$ holds inside $\widetilde G$. We now use the $\beta$-invariance: the involution $\sigma \in \mathrm{Gal}(F/F_0)$ (or the Hermitian-form involution on $V$) defining $G \subseteq \widetilde G$ as the fixed subgroup also preserves the decomposition above, because: - $\widetilde G_{E_i}$ is $\sigma$-stable (Stevens lattices are chosen $\sigma$-stable for $E_i \subseteq A \subseteq \mathcal B(G)$ inside $\mathcal B(\widetilde G)$); - $\widetilde N \cap G = N$ (the normaliser of the $\sigma$-fixed torus in $G$); - $\widetilde h \in \hat K_x \subseteq G$ is $\sigma$-fixed.

Applying Glauberman fixed-point averaging (Stevens:SS §3.6, specifically the $\sigma$-averaging used to pass from $\widetilde K_x$ to $K_x$): the components $(\widetilde h_1, \widetilde n, \widetilde h_2)$ can be replaced by their $\sigma$-symmetric versions $h_1 \in G_{E_1} = \widetilde G_{E_1} \cap G$, $n \in N = \widetilde N \cap G$, $h_2 \in G_{E_2}$, with $h_1 n h_2 = \widetilde h_1 \widetilde n \widetilde h_2 = \widetilde h \in G$.

Hence $\widetilde h \in G_{E_1} \cdot N \cdot G_{E_2} \subseteq \hat K_{E_1} \cdot N \cdot \hat K_{E_2}$ (since $G_{E_i} \subseteq \hat K_{E_i}$). After right-multiplying or left-multiplying by suitable elements of $N$, this lies in $N \cdot \hat K_{E_1} \cdot \hat K_{E_2} \cdot N$, establishing the desired inclusion. $\square$

Reference. The Glauberman fixed-point machinery used in Step 3 is [Stevens:SS §3.6]; the lattice-function and Iwahori-style factorization of $K_x$ for classical Stevens groups is [Stevens:SC §3.4] (intertwiner) and [Stevens:SS §3.5] (lattice sequence).

(c) Tame case (Kim–Yu–AFMO)

Lemma (tame A1). Let $G^{(0)} \subseteq \cdots \subseteq G^{(d)} = G$ be a twisted Levi tower with depth vector $\vec r = (r_0, \ldots, r_d)$, $K_x = G^{(0)}_{x, 0} \cdot J_x$ where $J_x = \prod_i G^{(i)}_{x, s_i}$ with $s_i = r_i/2$ ([[node:applications.tame_K_F_construction]]). Then $K_x$-local (A1) holds.

Proof via twisted-Levi Iwahori factorization. The key tool is the Iwahori-style factorization of $K_x$ along the twisted Levi tower ([Yu §15], [AFMO2 Lemma 3.6.12]): $$K_x \;=\; \prod_{i=0}^d \bigl( G^{(i)}_{x, s_i} \cap G^{(i+1)}_{x, 0+} \bigr) \;\cdot\; G^{(0)}_{x, 0},$$ where $s_0 := 0$ and $G^{(d+1)} := G$ formally. Each factor is a Moy–Prasad piece of a single twisted Levi group.

Step 1: parahoric (A1) for each $G^{(i)}$.

Each $G^{(i)}$ is itself a connected reductive group. The classical Bruhat–Tits (A1) for $\mathcal B(G^{(i)})$ gives: for any cells $E_1, E_2$ of $\mathcal B(G^{(i)})$ in a common apartment, $$G^{(i)} \;=\; G^{(i)}_{E_1} \cdot N^{(i)} \cdot G^{(i)}_{E_2}.$$

For $E_1, E_2 \subseteq A$ (the apartment of $G' = G^{(0)}$), each $E_j$ is a cell of $\mathcal B(G^{(i)})$ via the embedding $\mathcal B(G^{(0)}) \hookrightarrow \mathcal B(G^{(i)})$ — so (A1) applies inside each $G^{(i)}$.

Step 2: Moy–Prasad filtration intersection.

For each twisted Levi $G^{(i)}$ and each depth $s$, the Moy–Prasad groups $G^{(i)}_{x, s}$ have the standard Iwahori-Bruhat decomposition relative to any parahoric of $G^{(i)}$: $$G^{(i)}_{x, s} \;\subseteq\; N^{(i)} \cdot G^{(i)}_{E_1, s} \cdot G^{(i)}_{E_2, s} \cdot N^{(i)}$$ (refinement of Step 1 to depth-$s$ subgroups; this is standard [BT, §6.4]).

Step 3: combine across the tower.

Take $h \in K_x$. By the Iwahori factorization above, write $h = g_0 g_1 \cdots g_d$ with $g_i \in G^{(i)}{x, s_i} \cap G^{(i+1)}{x, 0+}$ (for $i < d$) and $g_d \in G^{(d)}{x, s_d} = G{x, s_d}$. Plus an overall $G^{(0)}_{x, 0}$ factor.

For each $g_i$, apply Step 2 inside $G^{(i)}$ at depth $s_i$: $$g_i \;\in\; N^{(i)} \cdot G^{(i)}_{E_1, s_i} \cdot G^{(i)}_{E_2, s_i} \cdot N^{(i)} \;\subseteq\; N \cdot \hat K_{E_1} \cdot \hat K_{E_2} \cdot N,$$ since $N^{(i)} \subseteq N$ (each twisted-Levi normaliser embeds in the apartment-system normaliser of $G$) and $G^{(i)}{E_j, s_i} \subseteq \hat K{E_j}$ (each Moy–Prasad piece at depth $s_i$ of any twisted Levi is in the full fixator of the cell).

For the $G^{(0)}_{x, 0}$ piece: this is in $G^{(0)}$, and apply Step 1 inside $G^{(0)}$ at depth 0 to get $G^{(0)}{x, 0} \subseteq G^{(0)} = G^{(0)}{E_1} N^{(0)} G^{(0)}{E_2} \subseteq N \hat K{E_1} \hat K_{E_2} N$.

Step 4: closure of the right-hand side under products.

The set $N \hat K_{E_1} \hat K_{E_2} N$ is closed under left/right multiplication by $N$. Iterating products of factors each in $N \hat K_{E_1} \hat K_{E_2} N$ (with the rearrangement that $\hat K_{E_2} \cdot N \cdot \hat K_{E_1}$ can be reduced using Step 1 applied to the parahorics $\hat K_{E_1}, \hat K_{E_2}$ inside the appropriate $G^{(i)}$), we obtain: $$h \;=\; g_0 g_1 \cdots g_d \;\in\; (N \hat K_{E_1} \hat K_{E_2} N)^{d+1} \;=\; N \hat K_{E_1} \hat K_{E_2} N$$ by iterated absorption. $\square$

Reference. The Iwahori factorization is [Yu §15] / [AFMO2 Lemma 3.6.12]; the Moy–Prasad Iwahori-Bruhat refinement (Step 2) is [BT, §6.4] (cf. [BS, Lem 3.1]).

Summary

The cases are summarised in the following table.

Case $K_x$ Key external input (A1) verification depth
(a) Parahoric $G_x$ classical Iwahori-Bruhat $G = G_{E_1} N G_{E_2}$ Trivial
(b) Classical $J^\circ(\beta, \Lambda_x)$ Stevens enveloping + Glauberman $\sigma$-averaging Moderate
(c) Tame $G^{(0)}_{x,0} \prod_i G^{(i)}_{x, s_i}$ twisted-Levi Iwahori factorization Substantial (combines tower)

In each case, the consequence is $C_0 = K_x \cdot A$ (combine with [[node:building.component_is_KxA]]), i.e.\ the connected component of $\BSigma$ containing $A$ is exactly the $K_x$-orbit of $A$.
