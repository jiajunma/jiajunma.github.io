Abstract cell complex

An abstract cell complex is a partially ordered set $(X, \preceq)$ together with a dimension function $\dim : X \to \mathbb{N}$ satisfying the following two axioms:

  • Strict monotonicity: for $E, F \in X$, if $E \preceq F$ and $E \neq F$, then $\dim E < \dim F$.
  • Face existence: every $F \in X$ with $\dim F > 0$ has a face of dimension $\dim F - 1$; that is, there exists $E \in X$ with $E \prec F$ and $\dim E + 1 = \dim F$.

The elements of $X$ are called cells and $\preceq$ is the face relation. For $E \preceq F$ we say $E$ is a face of $F$; when the containment is strict we write $E \prec F$.

The combinatorial data $(X, \preceq, \dim)$ is a direct abstraction of the face poset of a regular CW complex — Locally finite regular CW complex with cellular G-action — with all topology discarded. Strict monotonicity is the standard "dimension separates faces" property; face existence rules out pathological posets (e.g. a "2-cell" attached directly to a vertex with no intermediate edge), which are never face posets of regular CW complexes.

Downstream

Every downstream construction — sheaves and cosheaves (Sheaves and cosheaves on X as functor categories), the Alexandrov star topology (Alexandrov star topology on the face poset), the QFD and induction machinery (Quasi-fundamental domain, [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]), and the cellular chain complex ([[node:representations.chain_complex_C_dot]]) — is stated against this poset-with-dimension structure and does not use the topological realisation.

Finiteness of the face poset (finitely many faces, finitely many cofaces of each cell) is a separate condition, recorded as Locally finite abstract cell complex; likewise compatibility with a group action is a separate layer Cellular G-action on an abstract cell complex.

Lean formalization

class AbstractCellComplex (X : Type*) [PartialOrder X] where
  dim : X → ℕ
  dim_lt : ∀ {E F : X}, E ≤ F → E ≠ F → dim E < dim F
  exists_face_dim_pred :
    ∀ {F : X}, 0 < dim F → ∃ E : X, E < F ∧ dim E + 1 = dim F

AbstractCellComplex is a data-carrying class, not a Prop — the dimension function itself is genuine data and downstream files depend on it definitionally. The two axioms are the two bullets above.

Finiteness and $G$-equivariance are deliberately not fields of this class; they are separate typeclass layers so that lemmas requiring only the poset and dimension do not carry irrelevant hypotheses.
