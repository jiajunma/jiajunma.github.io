Alexandrov star topology

Let $X$ be an [[abstract cell complex|node:abstract_cell_complex.abstract_cell_complex]]. For each cell $E \in X$, the open star of $E$ is the up-set

$$ \mathrm{star}(E) := \{ F \in X : E \preceq F \}. $$

The Alexandrov star topology on $X$ takes a subset $U \subseteq X$ to be open iff $U$ is up-closed under $\preceq$; equivalently, iff $U$ is a union of stars. Under this topology:

  • each $\mathrm{star}(E)$ is open;
  • $E \in \mathrm{star}(E)$ for every $E$;
  • $\mathrm{star}(E)$ is the smallest open set containing $E$;
  • the family $\{\mathrm{star}(E) : E \in X\}$ is a basis for the topology.

The last two properties make the Alexandrov star topology the "right" topology for treating sheaves and cosheaves on the face poset as sheaves and cosheaves on a space. For a presheaf $\mathscr F$ on $X$, the value $\mathscr F(\mathrm{star}(E))$ is the stalk at $E$; the sheaf condition on the star basis reduces to a functoriality statement on the poset. This underpins the sheaf/cosheaf formalism of Sheaves and cosheaves on X as functor categories.

Lean formalization

def AbstractCellComplex.star (E : X) : Set X := {F | E ≤ F}

@[simp] theorem mem_star {E F : X} : F ∈ star E ↔ E ≤ F := Iff.rfl

theorem self_mem_star (E : X) : E ∈ star E := le_refl E

scoped instance : TopologicalSpace X where
  IsOpen U := ∀ ⦃E : X⦄, E ∈ U → star E ⊆ U
  isOpen_univ  := fun _ _ F _ => Set.mem_univ F
  isOpen_inter := fun _ _ hs ht _ hE _ hEF => ⟨hs hE.1 hEF, ht hE.2 hEF⟩
  isOpen_sUnion := fun _ hS _ hE _ hEF =>
    let ⟨t, ht, hEt⟩ := hE
    ⟨t, ht, hS t ht hEt hEF⟩

theorem isOpen_star (E : X) : IsOpen (star E) :=
  fun _ hE _ hF => le_trans hE hF

The topological-space instance is declared scoped so it activates only under open AbstractCellComplex. This prevents silent clashes when a downstream file wants to give $X$ a different topology (e.g. the geometric realisation of a CW complex).
