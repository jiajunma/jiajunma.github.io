The category $[X'/P]$ and its equivalence with the transporter category

The induction datum $(G', P)$ on $X'$ carries enough information to build a category on $X'$ intrinsically — no ambient $X = G \times^P X'$ required. This category is canonically equivalent to the transporter category $[X/G]$ of the induced space, and the equivalence immediately delivers the reduction of $G$-equivariant cosheaves on the induced space to functors on $X'$.

The category $]X'/P[$

Fix an [[induction datum|node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]] $(G', P)$ on $X'$ satisfying (P0)–(P2).

Definition ($]X'/P[$). The category $]X'/P[$ has:

  • objects: cells of $X'$;
  • morphisms: $$ \Hom_{]X'/P[}(E, E') \;=\; \{ nk \;:\; k \in P_E,\; n \in G',\; n \cdot E \preceq E' \}; $$
  • composition: the group law of $G$, well-typed because for $g = nk : E \to E'$ and $g' = n' k' : E' \to E''$, the composite $g' g = n' k' n k$ decomposes (using (P1) to push $k'$ across $n$) as $n'' k''$ with $n'' \in G'$, $k'' \in P_E$, and $n'' E = n' k' n E = n' n \cdot (n^{-1} k' n) E = n' n E$ (using $n^{-1} k' n \in P_{n^{-1} E'} \subseteq \Stab_G(n^{-1} E')$ etc.).

$[X'/P] := ]X'/P[^{\mathrm{op}}$ is the opposite category, with Hom-set $$ \Hom_{[X'/P]}(E, E') \;=\; \{ kn \;:\; k \in P_E,\; n \in G',\; E \preceq n \cdot E' \}. $$

The morphisms in $]X'/P[$ are elements of $G$ decomposed as $g = nk$ with $n \in G'$ and $k \in P_E$; the constraint is that $n$ (the "outer" $G'$-part) maps $E$ to a face of $E'$. The $P_E$-part acts trivially on $E$ (by ($P$3)-consequence: $P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(E)$ via Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space Lemma A(2) in the induced space), so it does not affect where $E$ lands — it is present as morphism data because it distinguishes different maps $E \to E'$ agreeing on $E$'s image.

Equivalence with the transporter category of the induced space

Theorem ($]X'/P[ \simeq ]X/G[$). Let $X = G \times^P X'$. The functor $$ \iota_* : \,]X'/P[\, \longrightarrow \,]X/G[ $$ sending $E \mapsto \iota E$ on objects and $g = nk \mapsto g$ on morphisms is an equivalence of categories. Dually, $[X'/P] \simeq [X/G]$.

Proof

Essential surjectivity. Every cell of $X = G \times^P X'$ is of the form $g \cdot \iota E$ for some $g \in G$, $E \in X'$ (ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clause (4)). For such a cell $q = g \cdot \iota E$: $g : \iota E \to q$ is an iso in $]X/G[$ with inverse $g^{-1} : q \to \iota E$, so $q$ is isomorphic to $\iota E \in \iota_*(]X'/P[)$.

Fully faithful. A morphism $\iota E \to \iota E'$ in $]X/G[$ is an element $g \in G$ with $g \cdot \iota E \preceq \iota E'$ in $X$. By clause (6) of ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) (or its K-decomposed form via Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space), this is equivalent to $\exists n \in G'$ with $n E \preceq E'$ and $g^{-1} n \in P_E$; equivalently $g = n k$ with $k = n^{-1} g \in P_E$ and $n E \preceq E'$. So $\Hom_{]X/G[}(\iota E, \iota E') = { nk : k \in P_E, n \in G', n E \preceq E' } = \Hom_{]X'/P[}(E, E')$ under $g = nk \leftrightarrow g$.

Hence $\iota_*$ is fully faithful. Combined with essential surjectivity, $\iota_*$ is a categorical equivalence. $\square$

Consequence: reduction of $\Cosh_G$ on the induced space

Composing the equivalence with the general functor-category identification $\Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \Fun([X/G], \mathscr C)$ (G-equivariant (co)sheaves as functors on ]X/G[ resp. [X/G]):

Corollary (reduction on the induced space). For $X = G \times^P X'$ and any category $\mathscr C$, $$ \boxed{\;\; \Cosh_G(G \times^P X',\, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}\bigl([X'/P],\, \mathscr C\bigr). \;\;} $$

No K-cosheaf, no main compatibility hypothesis, no $\mathrm{SRep}_G(\mathscr C)$-target category is needed for this statement. The category $[X'/P]$ is defined intrinsically from the induction datum $(G', P)$. All the "extra structure" of the K-cosheaf presentation is already packaged into the Hom-sets of $[X'/P]$.

Comparison with the general reduction

The general reduction $$ \Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \Fun([S]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C) $$ of Reduction of G-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD uses a QFD $S \subseteq X$ and passes through the full orbit subcategory $[S] \subseteq ]X/G[$.

In the induced-space case ($X = G \times^P X'$), the QFD is $S = \iota(X')$ (ι(X') is a quasi-fundamental domain of the induced space), and:

  • $[S] \subseteq ]X/G[$ is the full subcategory on cells of the form $\iota E$.
  • The equivalence $[X'/P] \simeq [X/G]$ restricts to an equivalence $[X'/P] \simeq [S]$ (both are supported on $\iota(X') \cong X'$).

So the induced-space specialisation of the general reduction is exactly the boxed corollary above. The $]X'/P[$ construction gives the reduction directly — without going through the ambient $X$, without invoking the K-cosheaf machinery. The general K-cosheaf-based reduction of Reduction of G-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD is the strictly more general statement, applicable to any QFD (not only those arising from an induction).

K-cosheaf unpacking specialised to the induced case

When one does want the K-cosheaf presentation (Reduction of G-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD secondary result), the induced-space case has an especially clean form:

  • Take $K := P$ (the induction cosheaf), viewed as a K-cosheaf on $\iota(X')$ via the automatic upgrade ($P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(\iota E)$ from Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space Lemma A(2)).
  • The main compatibility hypothesis $\Stab_G(E) = K_E \cdot \Fix_{G'}(E)$ is Lemma A(1) of the same node.
  • The resulting $\Cosh_{G', K}(\iota(X'), \mathscr C)$-form of the reduction is the paper's original statement of Prop 2.6.3 for the induced case.

Lean formalization

Fully formalized in SheafOnBuilding.InducedTransporterCat as of the current version. The category $]X'/P[$ and its equivalence with the transporter category of the induced space are packaged as:

The proof of IsEquivalence combines:

  • PXPrimeTransporterCat.toInducedTransporterCat_essSurj — essentially surjective, built from InducedSpace.exists_eq_smul_iota (every cell of the induced space is $G$-related to some $\iota E$) plus a two-morphism isomorphism $g \cdot \iota E \cong \iota E$ via $(g, g^{-1})$.
  • PXPrimeTransporterCat.toInducedTransporterCat_full + _faithful — the Mor subtype and the TransporterCat.Mor subtype coincide on underlying $G$- elements, so the map on Homs is definitionally the identity.

Consumed lemmas from earlier: GCellComplex.smul_le (composition), InducedSpace.exists_eq_smul_iota (ess. surj.), PartialOrder/AbstractCellComplex on InducedSpace d (functor target has a category structure — the scoped TransporterCat.category takes precedence because toInducedTransporterCat lives inside namespace SheafOnBuilding.TransporterCat).

Design note — why structure, not abbrev. TransporterCat uses abbrev TransporterCat G X := X with a scoped instance for its category. Trying the same pattern for PXPrimeTransporterCat fails: the object E : PXPrimeTransporterCat d unfolds to E : X', which already carries two competing Category X' instances — the preorder-derived one and TransporterCat.category (from abbrev TransporterCat G X' := X') — before ours is even considered. Adding a third scoped instance creates irresolvable ambiguity, and Hom in f : E ⟶ E' gets resolved to the wrong one at random. Wrapping in a structure gives a genuinely fresh type on which only our Category instance lives, sidestepping the whole issue. Round-tripping through .cell / .mk is cheap.

Deferred piece. The final reduction $\Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \Fun([X'/P]^{op}, \mathscr C)$ follows by composing:

  1. the categorical equivalence PXPrimeTransporterCat.toInducedTransporterCat_isEquivalence (done),
  2. the scaffolding funCatEquivOfQFD (already in SheafOnBuilding.TransporterFunEquiv), and
  3. the still-pending forward direction $\Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \Fun(]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C)$ of G-equivariant (co)sheaves as functors on ]X/G[ resp. [X/G].
