Face relation on the induced space

Fix the induction data $(G', P)$ satisfying (P0)–(P2) and the induced space $X = G \times^P X'$ of [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]], with $\iota : X' \hookrightarrow X$, $E \mapsto [(1, E)]$.

The face relation on $X$ has to be introduced by hand — $X$ is only a set-theoretic quotient — and its status as a partial order is not automatic.

Definition

Definition (face relation on $X$). For $x, y \in X$, $$ x \;\preceq\; y \iff \exists\, g \in G,\; \exists\, F \preceq E \text{ in } X',\quad x = [(g, F)] \text{ and } y = [(g, E)]. $$

In prose: $x \preceq y$ means $x$ and $y$ admit representatives $(g, F)$ and $(g, E)$ with the same first coordinate $g$ such that $F \preceq E$ in $X'$. Both sides of the definition are existentially quantified over the representative choice, so the definition is manifestly independent of representatives.

This is the generator obtained by pushing the face relation of $X'$ into $X$ via a common $G$-orbit: $$ g \cdot \iota F \;\preceq\; g \cdot \iota E \qquad (F \preceq E \text{ in } X'). $$ The class of pairs meeting the definition is precisely the image of this generator on the quotient.

No reflexive-transitive closure is needed. The ambient equivalence relation $\sim$ (which lets us shift representatives) absorbs all closure steps into a single generator on classes; that this single-step generator is already transitive on classes is proved as Theorem 2 of Well-definedness of the face relation on G ×^P X' (partial-order axioms) (using (P0), (P1)).

Reflexivity is built in. For any class $y = [(g, E)]$, take $F := E$; then $F \preceq E$ in $X'$ (reflexivity of the base poset), and $x := y = [(g, E)] = [(g, F)]$ witnesses $y \preceq y$.

Equivalent characterisation

Theorem 1 (single-witness form). For $(g_1, E_1), (g_2, E_2) \in G \times X'$, $$ [(g_1, E_1)] \;\preceq\; [(g_2, E_2)] \;\iff\; \exists\, n \in G',\quad n \cdot E_1 \preceq E_2 \text{ in } X' \;\wedge\; g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P_{E_1}. $$

Compare with the equivalence relation $\sim$ of [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]: replacing $\preceq$ on $X'$ by $=$ recovers $\sim$ verbatim. The face relation and the equivalence relation share the same syntactic shape.

Proof

($\Rightarrow$) Assume the definition: there exist $g \in G$ and $F \preceq E$ in $X'$ with $[(g_1, E_1)] = [(g, F)]$ and $[(g_2, E_2)] = [(g, E)]$. Unpacking the two $\sim$-equalities: $\exists n_1 \in G'$, $n_1 E_1 = F$, $g_1^{-1} g n_1 \in P_{E_1}$; and $\exists n_2 \in G'$, $n_2 E_2 = E$, $g_2^{-1} g n_2 \in P_{E_2}$. Combining $F = n_1 E_1$ with $F \preceq E = n_2 E_2$ gives $n_1 E_1 \preceq n_2 E_2$, i.e.\ $n_2^{-1} n_1 E_1 \preceq E_2$.

Take $n := n_2^{-1} n_1 \in G'$. The face-chain condition is done. For the residual $g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P_{E_1}$: substituting $g = g_1 p_1 n_1^{-1}$ (with $p_1 := g_1^{-1} g n_1 \in P_{E_1}$) and $g = g_2 p_2 n_2^{-1}$ (with $p_2 := g_2^{-1} g n_2 \in P_{E_2}$), $g_1^{-1} g_2 = p_1 n_1^{-1} n_2 p_2^{-1}$, and $$ g_1^{-1} g_2 n = p_1 \cdot n_1^{-1} (n_2 p_2^{-1} n_2^{-1}) n_1. $$ Now $n_2 p_2^{-1} n_2^{-1} \in P_{n_2 E_2} = P_E$ by (P1), and $F \preceq E$ gives $P_E \le P_F = P_{n_1 E_1}$ by (P0); finally $n_1^{-1} P_{n_1 E_1} n_1 = P_{E_1}$ by (P1). So the second factor lies in $P_{E_1}$; combined with $p_1 \in P_{E_1}$, the product is in $P_{E_1}$.

($\Leftarrow$) Assume the single-witness form with witness $n \in G'$. Take $g := g_2$, $F := n E_1$, $E := E_2$. Then $F \preceq E$ (given), and $[(g_1, E_1)] = [(g_2, F)]$ by $\sim$-witness $m := n$: $m E_1 = n E_1 = F$, and $g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P_{E_1}$ (given). $\square$

The single-witness form is the pre-quotient relation $\preceq_{\text{rep}}$ on $G \times X'$: $$ (g_1, E_1) \preceq_{\text{rep}} (g_2, E_2) \;:\iff\; \exists\, n \in G',\;\; n \cdot E_1 \preceq E_2 \;\text{and}\; g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P_{E_1}. $$ It is the form used in every downstream calculation.

The face relation is a partial order

The four axiom-driven proofs upgrading $\preceq_{\text{rep}}$ to a partial order on $X$ — reflexivity, transitivity, descent through $\sim$, antisymmetry modulo $\sim$ — are stated and proved in Well-definedness of the face relation on G ×^P X' (partial-order axioms). Axiom cost: (P0) + (P1) for transitivity and descent; dim preservation and strict monotonicity of $\dim$ for antisymmetry. (P2) does not enter.

Consequences

  • The embedding $\iota : X' \to X$, $E \mapsto [(1, E)]$, is order-preserving: for $F \preceq E$ in $X'$, the definition with $g := 1$ gives $\iota F \preceq \iota E$ immediately. Order reflection requires (P2); see ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clause (2).
  • The dimension function $\dim([(g, E)]) := \dim E$ is well-defined because $\sim$ forces $E_2 = n \cdot E_1$ for some $n \in G'$, and the $G$-action is dimension-preserving.
  • Together with local finiteness of $X'$ (inherited via $G$-translates) and the antisymmetry above, this equips $X$ with the abstract-cell- complex and $G$-cell-complex instances used by CW structure and QFD property on G ×^P X'.

Lean formalization

Bridge between math body and Lean

The math body's definition (symmetric same-$g$ form) and the Lean library's InductionLe (Theorem 1's single-witness form) are two presentations of the same relation on $G \times X'$. Theorem 1 is the equivalence. Lean takes the single-witness form as the operational definition — it makes each well-definedness lemma provable by direct group calculation. The name mirrors InductionRel (the pre-quotient equivalence relation from [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]): same syntactic shape, "$=$" replaced by "$\preceq$".

Well-definedness lemmas

The five well-definedness lemmas (reflexivity, transitivity, left descent, right descent, antisymmetry) and the assembled PartialOrder (InducedSpace d) instance are documented in the Lean formalization section of Well-definedness of the face relation on G ×^P X' (partial-order axioms).

Dimension and cell-complex instances

InducedSpace.dim realises $\dim([(g, E)]) := \dim E$ via Quotient.lift; well-definedness comes from GCellComplex.smul_dim (the $G$-action on $X'$ is dimension-preserving), and the compatibility rewrites are dim_iota, dim_smul.

The AbstractCellComplex (InducedSpace d) instance packages dim with dim_lt (proved by case-splitting on the InductionLe witness) and exists_face_dim_pred (lifted from $X'$ through the same-$g$ generator). Finally GCellComplex G (InducedSpace d) records the cellular-action compatibility: smul_le is invariance of InductionLe under simultaneous left translation, smul_dim is dim_smul.

Together with IsLocallyFinite (inherited via $G$-translates), this gives the full poset-level cell-complex structure on the induced space used by CW structure and QFD property on G ×^P X'.

Follow-up

A Relation.ReflTransGen-based reformulation of InductionLe (using the same-$g$ generator $g \cdot \iota F \preceq g \cdot \iota E$ for $F \preceq E$) plus an explicit equivalence lemma would let a reader match the body's Definition to a Lean object directly. Everything needed for the proof is already in place (transitivity + descent), so this is a well-scoped follow-up.
