$X'$ is a quasi-fundamental domain of $G \times^P X'$

Fix the induction data $(G', P)$ satisfying (P0)–(P2) and the induced space $X = G \times^P X'$ (cf.\ [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]). Identify $X'$ with $\iota(X') \subseteq X$.

Proposition.

  1. $\iota(X')$ is a face-closed subcomplex of $X$.
  2. The pair $(G', \iota(X'))$ makes $\iota(X')$ a [[quasi-fundamental domain|node:equivariant_sheaves.quasi_fundamental_domain]] of the $G$-space $X$; equivalently, the natural map $$G' \backslash [\![\iota(X')]\!] \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; G \backslash [\![X]\!]$$ is a bijection.
  3. Moreover $G' \le \Stab_G(\iota(X'))$; the sharp subgroup $G'$ is generally strictly smaller than the setwise stabiliser.
Proof

(1) Subcomplex. For face closure, take $q \preceq \iota E$ in $X$, with $E \in [\![X']\!]$. By clauses (4) and (6) of ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form), $q = g \cdot \iota F$ for some $g \in G$, $F \in [![X']!]$, and there exists $n \in G'$ with $n \cdot F \preceq E$ and $g^{-1} n \in P_E$. Then $g = n \cdot p^{-1}$ for some $p \in P_E$, so $q = n \cdot p^{-1} \cdot \iota F = n \cdot \iota F$ (using $P_E \subseteq \Fix_G(E)$ from Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space to see $p^{-1} \cdot \iota F = \iota F$ after face-closing to $E$), so $q = \iota(n \cdot F)$ by $G'$-equivariance. Thus $q \in \iota(X')$.

(2) QFD. Coverage is clause (4) of ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form): every cell of $X$ is a $G$-translate of some $\iota E$.

For injectivity on $G$-orbits, apply clause (5) with $g_1 = 1$: if $g \cdot \iota E = \iota F$ (both in $\iota(X')$) then there exists $n \in G'$ with $n \cdot E = F$ and $g n \in P_E \subseteq \Fix_G(E)$. Hence $E$ and $F$ are $G'$-equivalent through $n$, and we recover the orbit statement of the QFD definition.

(3) $G' \le \Stab_G(\iota(X'))$. For $n \in G'$ and $E \in [![X']!]$, clause (3) of ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) gives $n \cdot \iota E = \iota(n \cdot E) \in \iota(X')$. The reverse inclusion $n^{-1} \cdot \iota(X') \subseteq \iota(X')$ is symmetric. Strictness: if $g \in \Stab_G(\iota(X'))$ then $g \cdot \iota E = \iota F$ for some $F$, but $g$ need not lie in $G'$ — the residue is absorbed by $P_E$ (see (P4) in Recovering the induction data from a quasi-fundamental domain). $\square$

Downstream

Combined with the topological clauses of CW structure and QFD property on G ×^P X' (Hausdorffness, weak topology, regularity, characteristic maps — outside the abstract poset setting), this node closes the proof that $\iota : X' \hookrightarrow X$ is a QFD embedding.

The QFD-with-sharp-$G'$ statement is the input to the reverse direction of the induction/QFD correspondence (Recovering the induction data from a quasi-fundamental domain).
