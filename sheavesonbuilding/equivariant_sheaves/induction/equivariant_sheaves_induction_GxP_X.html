Induced space $G \times^P X'$ along a cosheaf $P$

Let $G' \le G$ act cellularly on $X'$, and let $P$ be a cosheaf on $X'$ of subgroups of $G$, $x \mapsto P_x$, satisfying:

  • (P1) $n P_x n^{-1} = P_{n \cdot x}$ for $n \in G'$;
  • (P2) $P_x \cap G' \subseteq \Fix_{G'}(x)$.

(P1) implies that $\Fix_{G'}(x)$ normalizes $P_x$.

Definition. The induced space $$X := G \times^P X' \;:=\; (G \times X') / \sim$$ where $$(g,x) \sim (h,y) \iff \exists n \in G' \text{ with } n \cdot x = y \text{ and } g^{-1}hn \in P_x.$$

The $G$-action is $g \cdot [(h,y)] = [(gh,y)]$ and $X$ carries the quotient topology.

Properties

  • Taking $P_x = \{e\}$ for all $x$ recovers the standard induced set $\mathrm{ind}_{G'}^G(X')$.
  • The construction depends on $P$ only up to the family $\Fix_{G'}(E) P_E$.
  • The map $\iota : X' \to X,\ x \mapsto [(1,x)]$ is injective.

After identifying $X'$ with $\iota(X')$, $X$ inherits a regular CW structure in which the $G$-action is cellular and $X'$ is a QFD for it (see CW structure and QFD property on G ×^P X').

Stabiliser formula

The stabiliser of a cell $\iota(E) \in X$ in $G$ factors as $\Fix_{G'}(E) \cdot P_E$, treated as its own node Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space. In particular $P_E \subseteq \Fix_G(E)$ for every cell $E$, which is what makes the face relation on $X$ well-defined after the quotient by $\sim$.

Lean formalization

The data $(G', P)$ with axioms (P1), (P2) — and additionally (P0) cosheaf monotonicity $E \preceq F \Rightarrow P_F \le P_E$, which is part of "$P$ is a cosheaf" — is packaged as

structure InductionDatum (X' : Type*) [PartialOrder X'] [AbstractCellComplex X']
    [MulAction G X'] [GCellComplex G X'] where
  G' : Subgroup G
  P  : X' → Subgroup G
  P0 : ∀ {E F : X'}, E ≤ F → P F ≤ P E
  P1 : ∀ (n : G), n ∈ G' → ∀ (E : X'),
    P (n • E) = (P E).map (MulEquiv.toMonoidHom (MulAut.conj n))
  P2 : ∀ (E : X') (m : G), m ∈ P E → m ∈ G' → m • E = E

The equivalence relation is

def InductionRel (X' : Type*) [...]
    (G' : Subgroup G) (P : X' → Subgroup G) :
    (G × X') → (G × X') → Prop :=
  fun ⟨g, E⟩ ⟨h, F⟩ => ∃ n ∈ G', n • E = F ∧ (g⁻¹ * h * n) ∈ P E

InductionRel.refl, InductionRel.symm, InductionRel.trans verify the equivalence-relation axioms; refl needs only unit-membership of subgroups; symm and trans both use (P1) to translate $P_{n \cdot E}$ back to $P_E$. The Setoid packaging is InductionRel.setoid; the induced space is

def InducedSpace (d : InductionDatum X') : Type _ :=
  Quotient (InductionRel.setoid d)

The canonical embedding is InducedSpace.iota, sending $E \mapsto [(1, E)]$. The $G$-action on InducedSpace is by left translation on the first factor.

(P0) does not enter the equivalence-relation axioms; it is needed for the face relation on the induced space (CW structure and QFD property on G ×^P X'). (P2) is used for the injectivity of $\iota$.
