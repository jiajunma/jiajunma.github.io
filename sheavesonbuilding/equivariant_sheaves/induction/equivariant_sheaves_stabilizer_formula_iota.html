Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space

Fix the induction data $(G', P)$ satisfying (P1) $nP_En^{-1} = P_{n \cdot E}$ for $n \in G'$ and (P2) $P_E \cap G' \subseteq \Fix_{G'}(E)$, and the induced space $X = G \times^P X'$ with cellular embedding $\iota : X' \to X,\ E \mapsto [(1, E)]$ (cf.\ [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]).

Recall the two subgroups attached to a cell (Cellular G-action on an abstract cell complex):

  • $\Stab_G(E) = \{g \in G : g \cdot E = E\}$ — setwise stabiliser;
  • $\Fix_G(E) = \bigcap_{F \preceq E} \Stab_G(F)$ — pointwise fixator (fix $E$ and every one of its faces).

Setwise formula at the cell level

Lemma A (setwise cell stabiliser).

  1. For every cell $E \in [\![X']\!]$ and every $g \in G$, $$\Stab_G(g \cdot \iota E) \;=\; {}^g\!\bigl(\Stab_{G'}(E) \cdot P_E\bigr).$$ In particular $\Stab_G(\iota E) = \Stab_{G'}(E) \cdot P_E$.
  2. $P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(\iota E)$ for every cell $E$.
Proof

By $G$-equivariance $\Stab_G(g \cdot \iota E) = g \Stab_G(\iota E) g^{-1}$, so treat $g = 1$.

($\supseteq$). Let $\hat g = u \cdot p$ with $u \in \Stab_{G'}(E)$, $p \in P_E$. Then $[(up, E)]_\sim = [(1, E)]_\sim$: apply the $\sim$-relation with witness $n := u \in G'$: $u \cdot E = E$ (since $u \in \Stab_{G'}(E)$), and $(up)^{-1} \cdot 1 \cdot u = p^{-1} \in P_E$. Hence $\hat g \in \Stab_G(\iota E)$.

($\subseteq$). Let $\hat g \in \Stab_G(\iota E)$. From the definition of $\sim$ applied to $(1, E)$ and $(\hat g, E)$ there exists $n \in G'$ with $n \cdot E = E$ (so $n \in \Stab_{G'}(E)$) and $\hat g^{-1} n \in P_E$, i.e.\ $\hat g \in n P_E^{-1} = n P_E$. By (P1), $\Stab_{G'}(E)$ normalises $P_E$ (as any $m \in \Stab_{G'}(E)$ conjugates $P_E$ to $P_{m \cdot E} = P_E$), so $n P_E = P_E n \subseteq \Stab_{G'}(E) \cdot P_E$.

Statement (2). For $p \in P_E$, apply Lemma A(1) with $u = 1$, $p = p$: $1 \cdot p = p \in \Stab_G(\iota E)$. $\square$

Pointwise formula on points (paper convention)

The setwise formula on cells is the poset shadow of the paper's pointwise formula on individual points:

Lemma B (paper's lem:Stab_gx). For $x \in X'$ identified with $\iota(x) \in X$ and $g \in G$, $$\Fix_G(g \cdot x) \;=\; {}^g\!\bigl(\Fix_{G'}(x) \cdot P_x\bigr).$$ Consequently $\hat K_x := \Fix_G(x) = \Fix_{G'}(x) \cdot P_x$. Also $P_E \subseteq \Fix_G(E)$ (pointwise) for every cell $E$.

For a point $x$, pointwise fix coincides with setwise stab on $\{x\}$, so Lemma B is Lemma A specialised to the singleton $\{x\}$ inside the CW closure of the cell containing $x$ — up to identifying $P_x$ with the cosheaf constant $P_E$ on the interior of $E$.

Pointwise formula at the cell level

Combining Lemma A with the definition of $\Fix_G$ as an intersection over subfaces:

$$ \Fix_G(\iota E) \;=\; \bigcap_{F \preceq E} \Stab_G(\iota F) \;=\; \bigcap_{F \preceq E} \Stab_{G'}(F) \cdot P_F. $$

Under the additional hypothesis that $F \mapsto \Fix_{G'}(F)$ is a sheaf on $X'$ (i.e.\ $F \preceq F' \Rightarrow \Fix_{G'}(F') \le \Fix_{G'}(F)$) and $F \mapsto P_F$ is a cosheaf (P0), the intersection telescopes:

$$ \Fix_G(\iota E) \;=\; \Fix_{G'}(E) \cdot P_E $$

recovering the paper's lem:Stab_gx at the cell level. The hypothesis $F \mapsto \Fix_{G'}(F)$ sheaf is a geometric input on the $G'$-action on $X'$, not automatic from the abstract poset setting.

Downstream

Lemma A(1) is used in the proof that $X'$ is a QFD of the induced space with the sharp subgroup $G'$ (ι(X') is a quasi-fundamental domain of the induced space). Lemma A(2) is used in the descent of the face relation to $\mathrm{InducedSpace}$ (Face relation on the induced space G ×^P X', ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form)).
