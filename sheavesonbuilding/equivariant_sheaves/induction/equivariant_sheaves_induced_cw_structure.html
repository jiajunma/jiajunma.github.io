CW structure and QFD property on $X = G \times^P X'$

We assume in addition that:

  • (F') $x \mapsto \Fix_{G'}(x)$ forms a cosheaf of subgroups of $G'$ on $X'$;
  • the $G'$-action on $X'$ is smooth (i.e.\ $\Fix_{G'}(E)$ is open compact in $G'$ for every cell $E$);
  • $P_E$ is open compact in $G$ for every cell $E$.

Set $[\![X]\!] := \{gE : g \in G,\ E \in [\![X']\!]\}$.

Key intermediate lemmas

The proof of the main proposition rests on four combinatorial building blocks, each treated as its own node:

  • Poset structure on $X$ (Face relation on the induced space G ×^P X'): the face relation descends through the quotient by $\sim$ to a well-defined partial order on $G \times^P X'$, using (P0), (P1), and dimension preservation of the cellular action for antisymmetry.

  • Cell equality on $X$ (ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clause (5)): $g_1 \cdot \iota E = g_2 \cdot \iota F$ iff $\exists n \in G'$ with $n \cdot E = F$ and $g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P_E$. This is the cell-intersection / QFD orbit lemma in the induction context.

  • Face relation on $X$ (ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clause (6)): the analogous statement with $\preceq$. Together with clauses (1)–(4) this equips $X$ with a $G$-cell-complex structure at the face-poset level.
  • Stabiliser formula (Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space): $\Fix_G(\iota E) = \Fix_{G'}(E) \cdot P_E$ and $P_E \subseteq \Fix_G(\iota E)$; the second inclusion makes the face relation on $X$ well-defined after descent through $\sim$.

Additionally the topology-level ingredients (Hausdorffness, closure finiteness with characteristic maps, weak topology, regularity) are proved directly in the topological setup below and are outside the poset-only formalisation.

The proposition

Proposition. There is a unique regular CW structure on $X = G \times^P X'$ for which $\iota : X' \hookrightarrow X$ is a cellular embedding inducing a homeomorphism onto its image, and the $G$-action is cellular. Moreover, $X'$ is a quasi-fundamental domain of the $G$-space $X$.

Proof

(full, 9 parts)

(a) $X$ is Hausdorff.

It suffices to show that the equivalence relation $\sim$ on $G \times X'$ is closed. Fix $(g, x), (h, y) \in G \times X'$ with $(g, x) \not\sim (h, y)$. We exhibit disjoint open neighbourhoods of their classes in $X$.

Let $E \in [\![X']\!]$ be the cell containing $x$ in its relative interior, and pick an open neighbourhood $V(E) \subseteq X'$ of $x$ that intersects only those cells $E''$ with $E \preceq E''$ (possible by local finiteness of $X'$). Since $P_E$ is open compact in $G$, the set $gP_E \times V(E)$ is an open neighbourhood of $(g, x)$.

The saturation of $gP_E \times V(E)$ under $\sim$ avoids $(h, y)$ provided $(g, x) \not\sim (h, y)$: if $(gp, x') \sim (h, y)$ for some $p \in P_E$, $x' \in V(E)$, then $\exists n \in G'$ with $n \cdot x' = y$ and $(gp)^{-1} h n \in P_{x'} \subseteq P_E$. Letting $x' \to x$ (using continuity + closedness of $\Fix_{G'}$-orbits), $(g, x) \sim (h, y)$, contradiction. Symmetric construction at $(h, y)$ gives the second neighbourhood.

(b) Cell decomposition.

Cells are $[\![X]\!] := \{gE : g \in G, E \in [\![X']\!]\}$. For each pair $(g, E)$ with $E \in [\![X']\!]$ and characteristic map $f_E : \sigma^{\dim E} \to X'$, define $\Phi_{g, E} := g \circ \iota \circ f_E$.

$\Phi_{g, E}$ restricted to $\mathring \sigma^{\dim E}$ is a continuous bijection onto $gE$ (using injectivity of $\iota$ + $G$-action by homeomorphisms). Image $\Phi_{g, E}(\sigma^{\dim E}) = g \cdot \iota(\overline E)$ is closed (Hausdorffness + compactness of $\sigma^{\dim E}$), so $\Phi_{g, E}$ is a closed map, i.e.\ a characteristic map.

(c) Closure-finiteness.

Each $\overline{gE} = g \cdot \iota(\overline E)$ meets only finitely many cells, since $\overline E \subseteq X'$ does (local finiteness of $X'$) and $G$-translates preserve incidence.

(d) Weak topology.

$U \subseteq X$ open iff each preimage $\Phi_{g, E}^{-1}(U)$ is open in $\sigma^{\dim E}$. Forward direction: continuity. Reverse: pull through the quotient map $\pi : G \times X' \to X$ ($\pi$ is open since $P_E$ open in $G$).

(e) Regularity.

$\Phi_{g, E} |_{\partial \sigma^{\dim E}}$ is a homeomorphism onto $g \cdot \iota(\overline E \setminus E)$ (regularity of $X'$ + injectivity of $\iota$).

(f) $\iota$ is a cellular embedding.

Combinatorial content: ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clauses (1)–(3). Topological content (that $\iota$ is also a topological embedding onto its image) follows from (a) + (e).

(g) $G$-action cellular.

$g \cdot (g' E) = (gg') E$, so $G$ permutes cells. Each cell-wise map is a homeomorphism (group action).

(h) $X'$ is a QFD.

Proved as X' is a quasi-fundamental domain of the induced space with the sharp subgroup $G'$ (not the setwise stabiliser of $\iota(X')$).

(i) Uniqueness.

Any CW structure with $\iota$ cellular + $G$ cellular forces the cell set to be $\{gE\}$. So the structure is unique. $\square$

Lean formalization

The topological content (Hausdorffness, weak topology, regularity, characteristic maps for clauses (a)–(e), (i)) is outside the abstract poset-level formalisation. The two poset-level clauses are split off:

  • Clauses (f) + (g) — the combinatorial embedding + $G$-cellularity — are the content of ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form), which discharges all six iota_* / smul_iota_* lemmas.
  • Clause (h) — the QFD structure — is X' is a quasi-fundamental domain of the induced space, which produces Subcomplex.ofIota, isQuasiFundDomain_subcomplexOfIota, and G'_le_subcomplexOfIota_stabilizer.

Only the packaging remains here: the PartialOrder, AbstractCellComplex, and GCellComplex instances on InducedSpace d are all in place, and dimension is available as InducedSpace.dim : InducedSpace d → ℕ (this is the only Lean declaration whose home is this node rather than one of the extractees).
