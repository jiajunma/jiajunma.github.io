Universal property of $G \times^P X'$ and induction by stages

Fix induction data $(G', P)$ on $X'$ satisfying (P1)–(P2) (P0 not needed for this node) and the induced space $X = G \times^P X'$ of [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]. Let $Y$ be any $G$-set. This node characterises $X$ by a universal property: the $G$-equivariant maps out of $X$ are exactly the "compatible" maps out of $X'$, in a precise sense that pins $X$ down uniquely up to canonical isomorphism.

Compatible maps

Definition ($G'$-and-$P$-compatible map). A map $f : X' \to Y$ is $(G', P)$-compatible if

  • ($G'$-equivariance) $f(n \cdot E) = n \cdot f(E)$ for every $n \in G'$ and $E \in X'$; and
  • ($P$-fix) $p \cdot f(E) = f(E)$ for every $E \in X'$ and $p \in P_E$, equivalently $P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(f(E))$.

Write $\mathrm{Map}_{G', P}(X', Y)$ for the set of $(G', P)$-compatible maps.

The two conditions coordinate cleanly: $G'$-equivariance says $f$ respects the $G'$-orbit structure of $X'$; the $P$-fix condition says the residual $P_E$-degrees of freedom (which the induction has already "quotiented away" in the definition of $\sim$) are killed by $f$ on the value side.

Universal property

Proposition. The assignment $f \longmapsto \widetilde f$ defined by $$ \widetilde f \bigl([(g, E)]\bigr) := g \cdot f(E) $$ is a bijection $$ \mathrm{Map}_{G', P}(X', Y) \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; \mathrm{Map}_G(X, Y), $$ natural in $Y$. The inverse sends $\Phi \in \mathrm{Map}_G(X, Y)$ to $f(E) := \Phi(\iota E)$.

Proof

Forward direction is well-defined. Given $f \in \mathrm{Map}{G', P}(X', Y)$, we must check that $g \cdot f(E)$ depends only on the class $[(g, E)]$. Suppose $(g_1, E_1) \sim (g_2, E_2)$: witness $n \in G'$ with $n \cdot E_1 = E_2$ and $g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P{E_1}$. Then $$ g_2 \cdot f(E_2) \;=\; g_2 \cdot f(n \cdot E_1) \;\stackrel{G'\text{-eq.}}{=}\; g_2 n \cdot f(E_1) \;=\; g_1 \cdot \bigl(g_1^{-1} g_2 n\bigr) \cdot f(E_1) \;\stackrel{P\text{-fix}}{=}\; g_1 \cdot f(E_1). $$ The first equality is $G'$-equivariance of $f$; the last uses $g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P_{E_1}$, i.e.\ $(g_1^{-1} g_2 n) \cdot f(E_1) = f(E_1)$.

Forward direction is $G$-equivariant. For $g' \in G$: $\widetilde f(g' \cdot [(g, E)]) = \widetilde f([(g' g, E)]) = (g' g) \cdot f(E) = g' \cdot \widetilde f([(g, E)])$.

Backward direction produces a compatible map. Given $\Phi \in \mathrm{Map}_G(X, Y)$, define $f(E) := \Phi(\iota E)$.

  • $G'$-equivariance of $f$: for $n \in G'$, $f(n \cdot E) = \Phi(\iota(n \cdot E)) = \Phi(n \cdot \iota E) = n \cdot \Phi(\iota E) = n \cdot f(E)$, using $\iota(n \cdot E) = n \cdot \iota E$ (ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clause (3), which is (P1)-based) and $G$-equivariance of $\Phi$.
  • $P$-fix: for $p \in P_E$, $p \cdot f(E) = p \cdot \Phi(\iota E) = \Phi(p \cdot \iota E) = \Phi(\iota E) = f(E)$, using $p \cdot \iota E = \iota E$ (Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space Lemma A(2), $P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(\iota E)$) and $G$-equivariance of $\Phi$.

The two maps are mutual inverses. Composing: - $(f \mapsto \widetilde f \mapsto \Phi \mapsto f')$: $f'(E) = \widetilde f(\iota E) = \widetilde f([(1, E)]) = 1 \cdot f(E) = f(E)$. - $(\Phi \mapsto f \mapsto \widetilde f \mapsto \Phi')$: $\Phi'([(g, E)]) = g \cdot f(E) = g \cdot \Phi(\iota E) = \Phi(g \cdot \iota E) = \Phi([(g, E)])$.

Naturality in $Y$ is verified by direct calculation on either formula. $\square$

Consequence: the induced space is unique up to canonical iso

The universal property characterises $G \times^P X'$ up to a unique $G$-equivariant isomorphism. Any other $G$-space $X''$ equipped with a $(G', P)$-compatible map $X' \to X''$ satisfying the same universal property is canonically identified with $G \times^P X'$ via the pair of forward and backward maps.

Induction by stages

Let $G'' \le G' \le G$, and suppose $P$ is a cosheaf of subgroups of $G$ on a $G''$-space $X''$ satisfying (P1)–(P2) for $G'' \le G$. Set $P'_E := P_E \cap G'$ (a cosheaf of subgroups of $G'$ on $X''$), and let $X' := G' \times^{P'} X''$. Extend $P$ to $X'$ by $\widetilde P_{[g', E]} := g' P_E (g')^{-1}$; well-definedness on $\sim$-classes uses (P1) for $P$.

Proposition (induction by stages). There is a canonical $G$-equivariant bijection $$ \Phi : G \times^{\widetilde P} \bigl(G' \times^{P'} X''\bigr) \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; G \times^P X'', \qquad \Phi\bigl([g,\, [g', E]]\bigr) = [g g', E]. $$

Proof sketch. Apply the universal property twice. For any $G$-set $Y$: $$ \mathrm{Map}_G(G \times^{\widetilde P}(G' \times^{P'} X''),\, Y) \;\cong\; \mathrm{Map}_{G', \widetilde P}(G' \times^{P'} X'',\, Y) \;\cong\; \mathrm{Map}_{G'', P}(X'',\, Y) \;\cong\; \mathrm{Map}_G(G \times^P X'',\, Y). $$ Both sides represent the same functor $Y \mapsto \mathrm{Map}_{G'', P}(X'', Y)$, so the corresponding representing objects are canonically isomorphic. Explicit calculation on classes confirms $\Phi([g, [g', E]]) = [g g', E]$. $\square$

Downstream

The stages identity is exactly what extends the generalised-building cosheaf $K$ from an apartment $A$ to the full Bruhat–Tits building of $G'$; see Generalized building B(Σ) = G ×^K A.

Lean formalization

The universal property is not yet formalised in SheafOnBuilding.Induction. The formalisation below is a design sketch — one that stays entirely within the abstract poset framework and requires no additional cell-complex structure on the target $G$-set $Y$.

Target types

The category of $(G', P)$-compatible maps is captured by a structure paralleling InductionDatum:

structure InductionMap (d : InductionDatum X') (Y : Type*)
    [MulAction G Y] where
  toFun : X' → Y
  /-- G'-equivariance: `f (n • E) = n • f E` for `n ∈ G'`. -/
  equivariant : ∀ {n : G}, n ∈ d.G' → ∀ (E : X'),
    toFun (n • E) = n • toFun E
  /-- Fix condition: every `p ∈ P E` fixes `f E`. -/
  fix_P : ∀ (E : X') (p : G), p ∈ d.P E → p • toFun E = toFun E

The category of $G$-equivariant maps $X \to Y$ is Mathlib's MulActionHom G X Y, notated X →[G] Y.

Forward direction: descend

def InductionMap.descend (f : InductionMap d Y) :
    InducedSpace d →[G] Y where
  toFun := Quotient.lift (fun gE : G × X' => gE.1 • f.toFun gE.2) <| by
    rintro ⟨g₁, E₁⟩ ⟨g₂, E₂⟩ ⟨n, hn_G, hn_smul, hn_P⟩
    -- Goal: g₁ • f E₁ = g₂ • f E₂
    -- Use f.equivariant hn_G to rewrite f E₂ = f (n • E₁) = n • f E₁
    -- Then use f.fix_P E₁ (g₁⁻¹ g₂ n) hn_P to absorb the residual.
    sorry
  map_smul' g q := by
    refine Quotient.inductionOn q ?_
    rintro ⟨h, E⟩
    show (g * h) • f.toFun E = g • (h • f.toFun E)
    rw [mul_smul]

The well-definedness computation is the paper's proof translated to Lean: from $g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P_{E_1}$ (in d.P E₁) and f.fix_P, $(g_1^{-1} g_2 n) \cdot f(E_1) = f(E_1)$; multiplying by $g_1$ on the left, $g_2 \cdot n \cdot f(E_1) = g_1 \cdot f(E_1)$; then apply f.equivariant hn_G to replace $n \cdot f(E_1)$ by $f(n \cdot E_1) = f(E_2)$.

Backward direction: restrict

def restrict (Φ : InducedSpace d →[G] Y) : InductionMap d Y where
  toFun E := Φ (iota d E)
  equivariant := by
    intro n hn E
    -- f (n • E) = Φ (ι (n • E)) = Φ (n • ι E) = n • Φ (ι E) = n • f E
    rw [← iota_smul_of_mem_G' d hn E, Φ.map_smul]
  fix_P := by
    intro E p hp
    -- p • Φ (ι E) = Φ (p • ι E) = Φ (ι E)
    rw [← Φ.map_smul p (iota d E),
        MulAction.stabilizer_iff.mp (P_le_stabilizer_iota d E hp)]

The G'-equivariance uses iota_smul_of_mem_G' (ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clause (3), Lean lemma iota_smul_of_mem_G'). The fix condition uses P_le_stabilizer_iota (Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space Lemma A(2)).

Assembly: the equivalence

def universalProperty : InductionMap d Y ≃ (InducedSpace d →[G] Y) where
  toFun := InductionMap.descend
  invFun := restrict
  left_inv := by intro f; ext E; simp [descend, restrict, iota]
  right_inv := by
    intro Φ
    ext q
    refine Quotient.inductionOn q ?_
    rintro ⟨g, E⟩
    -- (descend (restrict Φ)) ⟦(g, E)⟧ = g • Φ (ι E) = Φ (g • ι E) = Φ ⟦(g, E)⟧
    show g • Φ (iota d E) = Φ ⟦(g, E)⟧
    rw [← Φ.map_smul]
    rfl

The left_inv check is: (descend f) (ι E) = 1 • f E = f E. The right_inv check unfolds representative-wise and uses $g \cdot \iota E = [(g, E)]$ (which is by definition of the $G$-action on InducedSpace).

Axiom cost

Direction Axioms used
descend well-definedness (P1) via $f$'s $G'$-equivariance being verifiable on all reps
descend $G$-equivariance none (definitional)
restrict $G'$-equivariance (P1) via iota_smul_of_mem_G'
restrict fix Lemma A(2): $P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(\iota E)$ (no extra axiom)
left_inv / right_inv none

(P0) and (P2) are not needed for the universal property. This matches the paper: the universal property is a structural fact about the quotient by $\sim$, not about the face relation.

Induction by stages (further follow-up)

The induction-by-stages identity requires setting up the double induction $G \times^{\widetilde P}(G' \times^{P'} X'')$ in Lean, which is a fresh InductionDatum for the outer step. The cleanest route is to prove it via the universal property (two applications of universalProperty), avoiding an explicit combinatorial calculation on classes. Implementing this requires the universal property above to be in place first.
