    def descend (f : InductionMap d Y) : InducedSpace d →[G] Y where
  toFun := Quotient.lift (fun gE : G × X' => gE.1 • f.toFun gE.2) <| by
    rintro ⟨g₁, E₁⟩ ⟨g₂, E₂⟩ ⟨n, hn_G, hn_smul, hn_P⟩
    show g₁ • f.toFun E₁ = g₂ • f.toFun E₂
    -- Rewrite f E₂ = f (n • E₁) = n • f E₁.
    rw [← hn_smul, f.equivariant hn_G E₁, smul_smul]
    -- Now: g₁ • f E₁ = (g₂ * n) • f E₁.
    -- Use f.fix_P at residue r := g₁⁻¹ * g₂ * n ∈ P E₁.
    have h_fix := f.fix_P E₁ (g₁⁻¹ * g₂ * n) hn_P
    -- h_fix : (g₁⁻¹ * g₂ * n) • f E₁ = f E₁.
    -- Multiply by g₁ on the left, then simplify.
    have h_mul : g₁ • ((g₁⁻¹ * g₂ * n) • f.toFun E₁) = g₁ • f.toFun E₁ := by
      rw [h_fix]
    rw [smul_smul, show g₁ * (g₁⁻¹ * g₂ * n) = g₂ * n from by group] at h_mul
    exact h_mul.symm
  map_smul' g q := by
    refine Quotient.inductionOn q ?_
    rintro ⟨h, E⟩
    -- Both sides descend to (g * h) • f E.
    show (g * h) • f.toFun E = g • h • f.toFun E
    rw [mul_smul]

Universal property of $G \times^P X'$

Fix induction data $(G', P)$ on $X'$ satisfying (P1)–(P2) — (P0) is not needed for this node — and the induced space $X = G \times^P X'$ of [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]. Let $Y$ be any $G$-set. This node characterises $X$ by a universal property: the $G$-equivariant maps out of $X$ are exactly the "compatible" maps out of $X'$, in a precise sense that pins $X$ down uniquely up to canonical isomorphism.

Compatible maps

Definition ($G'$-and-$P$-compatible map). A map $f : X' \to Y$ is $(G', P)$-compatible if

  • ($G'$-equivariance) $f(n \cdot E) = n \cdot f(E)$ for every $n \in G'$ and $E \in X'$; and
  • ($P$-fix) $p \cdot f(E) = f(E)$ for every $E \in X'$ and $p \in P_E$, equivalently $P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(f(E))$.

Write $\mathrm{Map}_{G', P}(X', Y)$ for the set of $(G', P)$-compatible maps.

The two conditions coordinate cleanly: $G'$-equivariance says $f$ respects the $G'$-orbit structure of $X'$; the $P$-fix condition says the residual $P_E$-degrees of freedom (which the induction has already "quotiented away" in the definition of $\sim$) are killed by $f$ on the value side.

Universal property

Proposition. The assignment $f \longmapsto \widetilde f$ defined by $$ \widetilde f \bigl([(g, E)]\bigr) := g \cdot f(E) $$ is a bijection $$ \mathrm{Map}_{G', P}(X', Y) \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; \mathrm{Map}_G(X, Y), $$ natural in $Y$. The inverse sends $\Phi \in \mathrm{Map}_G(X, Y)$ to $f(E) := \Phi(\iota E)$.

Proof

Forward direction is well-defined. Given $f \in \mathrm{Map}{G', P}(X', Y)$, we must check that $g \cdot f(E)$ depends only on the class $[(g, E)]$. Suppose $(g_1, E_1) \sim (g_2, E_2)$: witness $n \in G'$ with $n \cdot E_1 = E_2$ and $g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P{E_1}$. Then $$ g_2 \cdot f(E_2) \;=\; g_2 \cdot f(n \cdot E_1) \;\stackrel{G'\text{-eq.}}{=}\; g_2 n \cdot f(E_1) \;=\; g_1 \cdot \bigl(g_1^{-1} g_2 n\bigr) \cdot f(E_1) \;\stackrel{P\text{-fix}}{=}\; g_1 \cdot f(E_1). $$ The first equality is $G'$-equivariance of $f$; the last uses $g_1^{-1} g_2 n \in P_{E_1}$, i.e.\ $(g_1^{-1} g_2 n) \cdot f(E_1) = f(E_1)$.

Forward direction is $G$-equivariant. For $g' \in G$: $\widetilde f(g' \cdot [(g, E)]) = \widetilde f([(g' g, E)]) = (g' g) \cdot f(E) = g' \cdot \widetilde f([(g, E)])$.

Backward direction produces a compatible map. Given $\Phi \in \mathrm{Map}_G(X, Y)$, define $f(E) := \Phi(\iota E)$.

  • $G'$-equivariance of $f$: for $n \in G'$, $f(n \cdot E) = \Phi(\iota(n \cdot E)) = \Phi(n \cdot \iota E) = n \cdot \Phi(\iota E) = n \cdot f(E)$, using $\iota(n \cdot E) = n \cdot \iota E$ (ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clause (3), which uses (P1)) and $G$-equivariance of $\Phi$.
  • $P$-fix: for $p \in P_E$, $p \cdot f(E) = p \cdot \Phi(\iota E) = \Phi(p \cdot \iota E) = \Phi(\iota E) = f(E)$, using $p \cdot \iota E = \iota E$ (Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space Lemma A(2), $P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(\iota E)$) and $G$-equivariance of $\Phi$.

The two maps are mutual inverses. Composing: - $(f \mapsto \widetilde f \mapsto \Phi \mapsto f')$: $f'(E) = \widetilde f(\iota E) = \widetilde f([(1, E)]) = 1 \cdot f(E) = f(E)$. - $(\Phi \mapsto f \mapsto \widetilde f \mapsto \Phi')$: $\Phi'([(g, E)]) = g \cdot f(E) = g \cdot \Phi(\iota E) = \Phi(g \cdot \iota E) = \Phi([(g, E)])$.

Naturality in $Y$ is verified by direct calculation on either formula. $\square$

Consequence: uniqueness up to canonical iso

The universal property characterises $G \times^P X'$ up to a unique $G$-equivariant isomorphism. Any other $G$-space equipped with a $(G', P)$-compatible map $X' \to \bullet$ satisfying the same universal property is canonically identified with $G \times^P X'$ via the pair of forward and backward maps.

Downstream

The universal property is the essential ingredient in Induction by stages: G ×^P̃(G' ×^P' X'') ≅ G ×^P X'': comparing $G \times^{\widetilde P}(G' \times^{P'} X'')$ with $G \times^P X''$ reduces (through three applications of the universal property) to a trivial identification of representing objects for the same functor $Y \mapsto \mathrm{Map}_{G'', P}(X'', Y)$.

Lean formalization

The universal property is fully formalised in SheafOnBuilding.Induction (namespace SheafOnBuilding.InducedSpace.InductionMap). The formalisation stays entirely within the abstract poset framework: the target $G$-set $Y$ needs no cell-complex structure, only [MulAction G Y].

Target types

The category of $(G', P)$-compatible maps is captured by a structure paralleling InductionDatum:

structure InductionMap (d : InductionDatum X') (Y : Type*)
    [MulAction G Y] where
  toFun : X' → Y
  /-- G'-equivariance: `f (n • E) = n • f E` for `n ∈ G'`. -/
  equivariant : ∀ {n : G}, n ∈ d.G' → ∀ (E : X'),
    toFun (n • E) = n • toFun E
  /-- Fix condition: every `p ∈ P E` fixes `f E`. -/
  fix_P : ∀ (E : X') (p : G), p ∈ d.P E → p • toFun E = toFun E

The category of $G$-equivariant maps $X \to Y$ is Mathlib's MulActionHom G X Y, notated X →[G] Y.

Forward direction: descend

def descend (f : InductionMap d Y) : InducedSpace d →[G] Y where
  toFun := Quotient.lift (fun gE : G × X' => gE.1 • f.toFun gE.2) <| by
    rintro ⟨g₁, E₁⟩ ⟨g₂, E₂⟩ ⟨n, hn_G, hn_smul, hn_P⟩
    show g₁ • f.toFun E₁ = g₂ • f.toFun E₂
    -- Rewrite f E₂ = f (n • E₁) = n • f E₁.
    rw [← hn_smul, f.equivariant hn_G E₁, smul_smul]
    -- Absorb the residue g₁⁻¹ g₂ n ∈ P E₁ via f.fix_P.
    have h_fix := f.fix_P E₁ (g₁⁻¹ * g₂ * n) hn_P
    have h_mul : g₁ • ((g₁⁻¹ * g₂ * n) • f.toFun E₁) = g₁ • f.toFun E₁ := by
      rw [h_fix]
    rw [smul_smul, show g₁ * (g₁⁻¹ * g₂ * n) = g₂ * n from by group] at h_mul
    exact h_mul.symm
  map_smul' g q := by
    refine Quotient.inductionOn q ?_
    rintro ⟨h, E⟩
    show (g * h) • f.toFun E = g • h • f.toFun E
    rw [mul_smul]

Backward direction: restrict

def restrict (Φ : InducedSpace d →[G] Y) : InductionMap d Y where
  toFun E := Φ (iota d E)
  equivariant := by
    intro n hn E
    show Φ (iota d (n • E)) = n • Φ (iota d E)
    rw [← iota_smul_of_mem_G' d hn E, MulActionHom.map_smul]
  fix_P := by
    intro E p hp
    show p • Φ (iota d E) = Φ (iota d E)
    have h_stab : p • iota d E = iota d E := P_le_stabilizer_iota d E hp
    rw [← MulActionHom.map_smul, h_stab]

The G'-equivariance uses iota_smul_of_mem_G' (ι : X' → G ×^P X' is a cellular embedding (poset form) clause (3), Lean lemma iota_smul_of_mem_G'). The fix condition uses P_le_stabilizer_iota (Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space Lemma A(2)).

Assembly: the equivalence

def universalProperty (Y : Type*) [MulAction G Y] :
    InductionMap d Y ≃ (InducedSpace d →[G] Y) where
  toFun := descend d
  invFun := restrict d
  left_inv f := by
    ext E
    exact descend_iota d f E
  right_inv Φ := by
    ext q
    refine Quotient.inductionOn q ?_
    rintro ⟨g, E⟩
    -- LHS: (descend (restrict Φ)) ⟦(g, E)⟧ = g • Φ (iota E)
    -- RHS: Φ ⟦(g, E)⟧
    show g • Φ (iota d E) = Φ (Quotient.mk _ (g, E))
    rw [← smul_iota, MulActionHom.map_smul]

The left_inv reduces to descend_iota (the iota case is the g = 1 case of descend_mk). The right_inv uses smul_iota (g • iota E = ⟦(g, E)⟧, from [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]) together with $G$-equivariance of $\Phi$.

(P0) and (P2) are not needed for the universal property. This matches the paper: the universal property is a structural fact about the quotient by $\sim$, not about the face relation. (The labels (P0), (P1), (P2) refer to fields of InductionDatum, not to Lean-kernel axiom declarations — the framework has none of the latter.)
