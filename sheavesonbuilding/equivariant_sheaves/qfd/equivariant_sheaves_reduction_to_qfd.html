Reduction of $G$-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD

Setting: $S \subseteq X$ is a [[quasi-fundamental domain|node:equivariant_sheaves.quasi_fundamental_domain]] for the $G$-action with distinguished subgroup $G' \le G$; $\mathscr C$ is a category (with enough structure to house cosheaves — the abstract poset framework demands only Category C). The proposition unfolds $G$-equivariant cosheaves on $X$ into $G'$-equivariant cosheaves on $S$ carrying additional stabiliser-action data.

The K-cosheaf of subgroups

The "additional data" on $S$ is a K-cosheaf: a family $K = {K_E}_{E \in S}$ of subgroups of $G$ (not of $G'$!) satisfying three conditions.

Definition (K-cosheaf on a QFD). A K-cosheaf on $S$ is a map $K : X \to \mathrm{Subgroup}(G)$ (meaningful only on cells of $S$; extended arbitrarily elsewhere) with:

  • ($K$-mono) Cosheaf functoriality. For $E \preceq F$ in $S$, $K_F \le K_E$. (Same direction as $\Fix_G$: bigger cell, smaller subgroup.)
  • ($K$-eq) $G'$-equivariance. For $n \in G'$ and $E \in S$, $K_{n \cdot E} = n K_E n^{-1}$.
  • ($K$-fix) Fixator dominance. For every $E \in S$, $K_E \le \Stab_G(E)$.

The paper's fourth axiom, $K_E \cap G' \subseteq \Fix_{G'}(E)$, is a consequence of ($K$-fix): $k \in K_E \cap G' \Rightarrow k \in \Stab_G(E)$ (by fix) $\Rightarrow k \cdot E = E$, hence $k \in \Fix_{G'}(E) = G' \cap \Stab_G(E)$. No separate hypothesis is needed.

The K-decomposed QFD orbit lemma

The bridge from the plain QFD orbit lemma (QFD orbit lemma: gE = hF iff there is n with nE=F and g⁻¹hn fixes E) to the K-cosheaf formalism requires one extra hypothesis on $K$ relative to the ambient stabiliser data:

Main compatibility hypothesis. For every $E \in S$, $$\Stab_G(E) = K_E \cdot \Fix_{G'}(E).$$ Equivalently: every $f \in \Stab_G(E)$ factors as $f = k \cdot m$ with $k \in K_E$ and $m \in \Fix_{G'}(E) = G' \cap \Stab_G(E)$.

Under this hypothesis, the plain QFD orbit lemma refines:

Lemma (K-decomposed QFD orbit lemma). For $E, F \in S$ and $g, h \in G$: $$ g \cdot E = h \cdot F \iff \exists\, n \in G',\; n \cdot E = F \wedge g^{-1} h n \in K_E. $$

Proof

($\Rightarrow$) By the plain QFD orbit lemma, $\exists n_0 \in G'$ with $n_0 E = F$ and $g^{-1} h n_0 \in \Stab_G(E)$. Apply the main compatibility hypothesis: $g^{-1} h n_0 = k \cdot m$ with $k \in K_E$ and $m \in \Fix_{G'}(E) = G' \cap \Stab_G(E)$. Set $n := n_0 m^{-1} \in G'$. Then $n \cdot E = n_0 m^{-1} \cdot E = n_0 \cdot E = F$ (since $m$ fixes $E$), and $g^{-1} h n = (k m) m^{-1} = k \in K_E$.

($\Leftarrow$) From $g^{-1} h n \in K_E \subseteq \Stab_G(E)$, $(g^{-1} h n) \cdot E = E$, so $g \cdot E = h n \cdot E = h \cdot F$. $\square$

A second useful form gives the K-decomposition of the "reachable-by-$G$" face relation on $S$:

Lemma (K-decomposed Hom-set for $]X/G[$). For $E, E' \in S$ and $g \in G$: $$ g \cdot E \preceq E' \iff \exists\, n, k \in G,\; n \in G',\; k \in K_E,\; n \cdot E \preceq E',\; g = n k. $$

Proof. Apply the K-decomposed orbit lemma to $g \cdot E = 1 \cdot (g \cdot E)$ with $F := g \cdot E$: extract $n \in G'$ with $n \cdot E = g \cdot E$ and $g^{-1} n \in K_E$; take $k := n^{-1} g$. The ($\Leftarrow$) direction uses ($K$-fix) directly. $\square$

The orbit category $]X/G[$ and the first equivalence

Definition (orbit category $]X/G[$). Objects: cells of $X$. Morphisms $E \to E'$: elements $g \in G$ with $g \cdot E \preceq E'$. Composition is group multiplication in reverse order.

Endomorphisms in $]X/G[$ are exactly the stabiliser: an endomorphism $g \in \Hom_{]X/G[}(E, E)$ has $g \cdot E \preceq E$, and dimension preservation ($G$-action is cellular) + strict monotonicity of $\dim$ on proper faces forces $g \cdot E = E$, so $g \in \Stab_G(E)$. This identification is a monoid isomorphism $\End_{]X/G[}(E) \cong \Stab_G(E)$.

The full subcategory on cells of $S \subseteq X$ is denoted $[S]_{]X/G[}$.

Proposition (first equivalence). If $S$ is a QFD for $G' \le G$, the inclusion functor $$ \iota : [S]_{]X/G[} \;\longrightarrow\; ]X/G[ $$ is an equivalence of categories.

Proof. Full-subcategory inclusions are always fully faithful. Essential surjectivity: any cell $E \in X$ has, by QFD surjectivity, a $g \in G$ and $F \in S$ with $g \cdot F = E$; then $g \in \Hom_{]X/G[}(F, E)$ and $g^{-1} \in \Hom_{]X/G[}(E, F)$ are mutually inverse isomorphisms. $\square$

Passing to functor categories: for any $\mathscr C$, $$ \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}([S]_{]X/G[}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C). $$

The reduction proposition

Combining the categorical equivalence with the K-cosheaf interpretation of the extra "action" data on the target side:

Proposition (reduction of equivariant cosheaves to a QFD). Let $K$ be a K-cosheaf on $S$ satisfying the main compatibility hypothesis $\Stab_G(E) = K_E \cdot \Fix_{G'}(E)$ for every $E \in S$. Then there are canonical equivalences of categories $$ \Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}([S]_{]X/G[}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \Cosh_{G', K}(S, \mathscr C), $$ where $\Cosh_{G', K}(S, \mathscr C)$ is the category of $G'$-equivariant cosheaves on $S$ valued in the subgroup-representation category $\mathrm{SRep}_G(\mathscr C)$, whose $E$-component $(V_E, a^{V_E})$ is a $K_E$-representation and whose morphism data intertwines the action of $\Stab_G(E) \cap K_E$ correctly.

Proof sketch.

  • First equivalence. Equivariant cosheaves on $X$ are functors $]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathscr C$ (with stalk action recorded on endomorphisms via the isomorphism $\End_{]X/G[}(E) \cong \Stab_G(E)$). Restricting along the inclusion $\iota : [S]_{]X/G[} \hookrightarrow ]X/G[$ — an equivalence by the first equivalence proposition — gives an equivalence of functor categories.

  • Second equivalence. Unpack the resulting functor $[S]_{]X/G[}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathscr C$ into $G'$-cosheaf data on $S$ (the poset-face part) together with a stabiliser action (the $\End(E) \cong \Stab_G(E)$ part). The stabiliser action factors through $K_E$ (via ($K$-fix): $K_E \le \Stab_G(E)$). Reconstruction uses the K-decomposed Hom-set: for $g : E \to E'$, canonically decompose $g = n k$ with $n \in G'$ and $k \in K_E$, then set $F(g) = a^{V_{E'}}_k \circ r^V_{n E, E'} \circ a^V_{n, E}$.

The two equivalences compose to give the full reduction. $\square$

Downstream

This proposition is the keystone for §4 (Heisenberg–Weil equivalence) and §8 (reduction to depth zero): equivariant data on the full generalised building $\BSigma$ can be checked on the apartment $A$. See Res and Sat functors between Coeff_G(B(Σ), Φ) and Coeff_M⁰(B(M)).

Lean formalization

The reduction is a large body of code split across five modules; the pieces are integrated but the outermost equivalence $\Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \Cosh_{G', K}(S, \mathscr C)$ is partially formalised — the categorical scaffolding (orbit category, QFD-driven functor-category equivalence, K-decomposed Hom-set lemma, $\mathrm{SRep}_G(\mathscr C)$ target category) is in place; the final identification of $\Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C)$ with $\mathrm{Fun}(]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C)$ is in progress in GCosheafToFun and its downstream files.
