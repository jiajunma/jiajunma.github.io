Reduction of $G$-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD

Setting: $G$ acts cellularly on a cell complex $X$, and $S \subseteq X$ is a [[quasi-fundamental domain|node:equivariant_sheaves.quasi_fundamental_domain]] with distinguished subgroup $G' \le G$. $\mathscr C$ is any category.

The reduction theorem describes $G$-equivariant cosheaves on $X$ as functors on a small full subcategory of the transporter category $]X/G[$ supported on cells of $S$.

The transporter category $]X/G[$ and the subcategory $[S]$

The transporter category $]X/G[$ is defined in The transporter categories ]X/G[ and [X/G]: objects are cells of $X$, morphisms $E \to E'$ are elements $g \in G$ with $g \cdot E \preceq E'$, and endomorphisms recover the stabiliser ($\End_{]X/G[}(E) \cong \Stab_G(E)$). The opposite category $[X/G] := ]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}}$ is what G-equivariant (co)sheaves as functors on ]X/G[ resp. [X/G] uses to state the equivalences $\Sh_G \simeq \Fun(]X/G[, \mathscr C)$ and $\Cosh_G \simeq \Fun([X/G], \mathscr C)$.

Definition ($[S]$). The full subcategory of $]X/G[$ on cells of the QFD $S$: $$ [S] \;:=\; \{\, E \in ]X/G[ \;:\; E \in S \,\} \;\subseteq\; ]X/G[. $$

The reduction theorem

Theorem (reduction of $G$-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD). Let $G$ act cellularly on $X$ and let $S \subseteq X$ be a QFD with distinguished subgroup $G'$. For any category $\mathscr C$, there is a canonical equivalence of categories $$ \boxed{\;\; \Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}\bigl([S]^{\mathrm{op}},\, \mathscr C\bigr). \;\;} $$

The theorem's content is that $G$-equivariant cosheaves on $X$ are determined by their restriction to cells of $S$ together with the data of how those restrictions transform under morphisms in $[S]$. No K-cosheaf is needed for this statement; the only hypothesis is that $S$ is a QFD.

Proof

Factor the equivalence through the full transporter category: $$ \Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \;\underset{(1)}{\simeq}\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C) \;\underset{(2)}{\simeq}\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}([S]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C). $$

(1) is the general identification "$G$-equivariant cosheaves on $X$ are functors on the opposite of the transporter category" — a structural fact about $\Cosh_G$, independent of any QFD. A functor $W : ]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathscr C$ carries, for each cell $E \in X$, the stalk $W_E := W(E)$ together with the induced endomorphism action $\End_{]X/G[}(E) \cong \Stab_G(E) \to \Aut W_E$ (the equivariant-structure data), and for each face relation the restriction morphism. The two data lines are exactly what a $G$-equivariant cosheaf carries.

(2) is the induced-equivalence-of-functor-categories along the inclusion $\iota : [S] \hookrightarrow ]X/G[$. This inclusion is a categorical equivalence because $S$ is a QFD:

  • Fully faithful. Automatic for full-subcategory inclusions.
  • Essentially surjective. Given any cell $E \in X$, the QFD's surjectivity axiom yields $g \in G$ and $F \in S$ with $g \cdot F = E$; then $g \in \Hom_{]X/G[}(F, E)$ and $g^{-1} \in \Hom_{]X/G[}(E, F)$ are mutually inverse isomorphisms.

Precomposition with an equivalence of small categories gives an equivalence of functor categories, so (2) holds.

Combining (1) and (2): $\Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}([S]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C)$. $\square$

Hypotheses used

Just the QFD hypothesis, unpacked:

  • $S$ is face-closed (built into Subcomplex X);
  • $G' \le \Stab_G(S)$ (($G'$-le-stab));
  • every $G$-orbit on $X$ meets $S$ ((Surjectivity)) — this is what makes $\iota : [S] \hookrightarrow ]X/G[$ essentially surjective;
  • (Orbit relation) is not used for the reduction theorem itself, though it does enter the K-cosheaf unpacking below.

No K-cosheaf, no main compatibility hypothesis $\Stab_G(E) = K_E \cdot \Fix_{G'}(E)$, no $\mathrm{SRep}_G(\mathscr C)$ target category — those enter only when one wants to unpack the functor category $\Fun([S]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C)$ into a more concrete "cosheaf-with-representation-data" form (see below).

K-cosheaf unpacking (secondary result)

Applications in §4 (Heisenberg–Weil) and §8 (depth zero) prefer a different presentation of $\Fun([S]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C)$ in terms of a K-cosheaf of subgroups. This is a reformulation of the target category, not a strengthening of the reduction theorem.

The K-cosheaf structure

Definition (K-cosheaf on a QFD). A K-cosheaf on $S$ is a map $K : X \to \mathrm{Subgroup}(G)$ (meaningful only on cells of $S$; extended arbitrarily elsewhere) with:

  • ($K$-mono) $E \preceq F$ in $S$ $\Rightarrow$ $K_F \le K_E$.
  • ($K$-eq) $K_{n \cdot E} = n K_E n^{-1}$ for $n \in G'$ and $E \in S$.
  • ($K$-fix) $K_E \le \Stab_G(E)$ for every $E \in S$.

Under the additional main compatibility hypothesis $$ \Stab_G(E) \;=\; K_E \cdot \Fix_{G'}(E) \qquad \text{for every } E \in S, $$ the K-decomposed QFD orbit lemma (orbit_iff_K in Lean, Sheaf7 lem:qfd_surj_ext) refines the plain QFD orbit lemma (QFD orbit lemma: gE = hF iff there is n with nE=F and g⁻¹hn fixes E): $$ g \cdot E = h \cdot F \iff \exists\, n \in G',\;\; n \cdot E = F \wedge g^{-1} h n \in K_E. $$ Applied to $g \cdot E = 1 \cdot (g \cdot E)$, this yields the Hom-set decomposition (qfd_equiv_hom, lem:qfd_equiv): $$ g \cdot E \preceq E' \iff \exists\, n, k \in G,\; n \in G',\; k \in K_E,\; n \cdot E \preceq E',\; g = n k. $$ Both are the raw material for the reformulation.

The reformulation

Fixing such a $K$ satisfying the main compatibility hypothesis:

Definition ($\Cosh_{G', K}(S, \mathscr C)$). The full subcategory of $\Cosh_{G'}(S, \mathrm{SRep}_G(\mathscr C))$ whose objects are specified by the following data + four compatibility conditions.

Data. For each $E \in S$:

  • $V_E \in \mathscr C$ and a group homomorphism $a^{V_E} : K_E \to \Aut_\mathscr{C}(V_E)$;
  • for each $E' \preceq E$ in $S$, a morphism $r^V_{E, E'} : V_E \to V_{E'}$;
  • for each $g \in G'$ and $E \in S$, an isomorphism $a^V_{g, E} : V_E \to V_{g \cdot E}$.

Compatibility.

  1. $(V_E, a^{V_E})$ is a $K_E$-representation.
  2. $r^V_{E, E'}$ is $a^{V_E}_k$-equivariant for $k \in K_E$, $E' \preceq E$.
  3. $a^V_{g, E} \circ a^{V_E}k = a^{V{g \cdot E}}{gkg^{-1}} \circ a^V{g, E}$ for $g \in G'$, $k \in K_E$.
  4. Gluing. $a^{V_E}_k = a^V_{k, E}$ for $k \in K_E \cap G'$ — the two nominally separate action structures agree on the overlap.

Proposition (K-cosheaf unpacking). Under the setup above, $$ \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}([S]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \Cosh_{G', K}(S, \mathscr C). $$

Proof (⇒). Given $F : [S]^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathscr C$, extract $V_E := F(E)$ with endomorphism-action factored through $K_E \le \Stab_G(E)$; extract $r^V$ from face-relation morphisms; extract $a^V_g$ from $g \in G'$-morphisms. Conditions 1–3 come from $F$-functoriality. Condition 4 comes from $F$-functoriality applied to elements of $K_E \cap G'$ (which admit two morphism-in-$[S]$ representatives, both of which $F$ must send to the same morphism).

Proof (⇐). Given $V \in \Cosh_{G', K}(S, \mathscr C)$, define $F(g)$ for $g : E \to E'$ in $[S]$ by decomposing $g = n \cdot k$ via qfd_equiv_hom, then $$F(g) := a^V_{n, E} \circ r^V_{n \cdot E, E'} \circ (a^{V_E}_k)^{-1}.$$ Independence of the choice $(n, k)$ is exactly condition 4 (gluing). Functoriality is a direct computation.

Composing the two equivalences:

Corollary (paper's combined statement). Under the QFD hypothesis plus the K-cosheaf $K$ satisfying the main compatibility hypothesis, $$ \Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \Cosh_{G', K}(S, \mathscr C). $$

This corollary is Sheaf7 prop:res_cosheaf_QFD (Prop 2.6.3 / 2.7.2) in its combined form.

Specialisation to the induced-space case

When $S$ arises from an induction datum $(G', P)$ — that is, $X = G \times^P X'$ and $S = \iota(X')$ (ι(X') is a quasi-fundamental domain of the induced space) — the reduction admits a strictly more concrete presentation that avoids the K-cosheaf detour:

$$\Cosh_G(G \times^P X', \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \Fun([X'/P], \mathscr C),$$

where $[X'/P]$ is a category defined directly on $X'$ with morphism sets $\{nk : n \in G',\ k \in P_E,\ nE \preceq E'\}$ (intrinsic to the induction datum, no K-cosheaf, no main compatibility hypothesis). See The category [X'/P] and its equivalence with the transporter category of G ×^P X' for the category $[X'/P]$, the equivalence $[X'/P] \simeq [X/G]$ that underlies this specialisation, and the comparison with the general K-cosheaf reduction.

Downstream

The clean reduction $\Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \Fun([S]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C)$ is the keystone for §4 (Heisenberg–Weil equivalence) and §8 (reduction to depth zero): equivariant data on the full generalised building $\BSigma$ is determined by its restriction to an apartment $A$. See Res and Sat functors between Coeff_G(B(Σ), Φ) and Coeff_M⁰(B(M)). The K-cosheaf presentation is what makes the connection with representation theory on the RHS explicit.

Relationship between $K$ and $P$

The K-cosheaf here and the induction cosheaf $P$ on the induction base $X'$ (see [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]]) have the same shape — both are functions cell $\to$ subgroup of $G$ — but their hypotheses differ.

Hypothesis Induction ($P$ on $X'$) Reduction ($K$ on $S$)
Monotonicity ($P$0) $E \preceq F \Rightarrow P_F \le P_E$ ($K$-mono) $E \preceq F \Rightarrow K_F \le K_E$
$G'$-equivariance ($P$1) $nP_En^{-1} = P_{n \cdot E}$ ($K$-eq) $K_{n \cdot E} = nK_En^{-1}$
Stabiliser relation ($P$2) $P_E \cap G' \subseteq \Fix_{G'}(E)$ ($K$-fix) $K_E \le \Stab_G(E)$

Rows 1–2 coincide; row 3 differs. ($K$-fix) is strictly stronger than ($P$2): the former says every $K$-element fixes $E$, the latter only says the $G'$-intersection does. ($K$-fix) $\Rightarrow$ ($P$2) but the converse fails in general.

Coincidence in the induced-space setting. When $X = G \times^P X'$ is the induced space of an [[induction datum|node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]], the image $S := \iota(X') \subseteq X$ is the sharp QFD (ι(X') is a quasi-fundamental domain of the induced space), and the stabiliser formula (Cell-stabiliser and fixator formulas on the induced space Lemma A(2)) gives $P_E \subseteq \Stab_G(\iota E)$ — precisely ($K$-fix) on $\iota(X')$. So the induction cosheaf $P$ automatically upgrades to a K-cosheaf on $S$, and the main compatibility hypothesis becomes Lemma A(1).

The reduction theorem in Lean

The clean form has two ingredients:

  • Cosh_G(X, 𝒞) ≃ Fun(]X/G[ᵒᵖ, 𝒞). The general functor-versus- cosheaf identification. In progress in SheafOnBuilding.GEquivCosheafToFun; the current building blocks are GEquivCosheaf'.mapOfG and mapOfG_one.
  • Fun([S]ᵒᵖ, 𝒞) ≃ Fun(]X/G[ᵒᵖ, 𝒞). Already formalised as SheafOnBuilding.TransporterCat.funCatEquivOfQFD (via ιOnSubcomplex_isEquivalence).

Composing the two once the first is complete gives the reduction theorem.
