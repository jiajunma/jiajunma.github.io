QFD orbit lemma

Let $X' \subseteq X$ be a QFD for $G$ with stabilizing subgroup $G'$.

Lemma. The action map $G \times [\![X']\!] \to [\![X]\!]$, $(g,E) \mapsto gE$ is surjective. For $E,F \in [\![X']\!]$ and $g,h \in G$, the equality $gE = hF$ holds if and only if there exists $n \in G'$ with $nE = F$ and $g^{-1}hn \in \Fix_G(E)$.

Proof

Surjectivity is immediate from the QFD bijection $G' \backslash [\![X']\!] \cong G \backslash [\![X]\!]$. For the second clause: if $gE = hF$ then $E$ and $F$ lie in the same $G$-orbit; by the QFD bijection, some $n \in G'$ satisfies $nE = F$, and $gE = hF = hnE$ forces $g^{-1}hn \in \Stab_G(E) = \Fix_G(E)$. The converse is immediate. $\square$

This lemma is the technical backbone for reducing $G$-equivariant data on $X$ to $G'$-equivariant data on $X'$; see Reduction of G-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD.

Variant with a subgroup factorization

If $\{P_E\}_{E \in [\![X']\!]}$ is a family of subgroups with $nP_En^{-1} = P_{nE}$ and $\Fix_{G'}(E) P_E = \Fix_G(E)$, then $gE = hF$ iff there is $n \in G'$ with $nE = F$ and $g^{-1}hn \in P_E$. This refined form is the input to the induction construction [[node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_GxP_X]].

Lean formalization

The plain form is IsQuasiFundDomain.orbit_eq_iff:

theorem orbit_eq_iff (G' : Subgroup G) (S : Subcomplex X)
    [IsQuasiFundDomain G' S]
    {E F : X} (hE : E ∈ S) (hF : F ∈ S) (g h : G) :
    g • E = h • F ↔
      ∃ n ∈ G',
        n • E = F ∧ (g⁻¹ * h * n) ∈ MulAction.stabilizer G E

The (→) direction is the orbit_iff clause of IsQuasiFundDomain re-expressed with $\Fix_G(E)$ read as MulAction.stabilizer G E; the (←) direction is a direct group-action calculation with no QFD hypothesis.

The refined form with an explicit family $\{P_E\}$ requires an [[induction datum|node:equivariant_sheaves.induction_data_reverse]] InductionData G' S and appears as InductionData.orbit_eq_iff: the forward direction reuses orbit_eq_iff and absorbs the $\Fix_G$ residue via the $\mathrm{P4}$ factorisation; the backward direction uses P3 and $G$-equivariance.

Equivariance of $\Fix_G$ under $G$-conjugation, needed above, is MulAction.stabilizer_smul_eq_stabilizer_map_conj from Mathlib; a project-local specialisation is exposed as IsQuasiFundDomain.stabilizer_smul.

KData-based variant (Sheaf7 lem:qfd_surj_ext + lem:qfd_equiv)

The refined form using a $K$-cosheaf-of-subgroups is also available through SheafOnBuilding.KData (bundling K : X → Subgroup G with monotone/equivariance/fixator-dominance axioms — the input to Sheaf7 Prop 2.7.2). Two theorems are stated there:

  • IsQuasiFundDomain.orbit_iff_K — the K-decomposed orbit lemma (lem:qfd_surj_ext): under the main hypothesis $\Fix_G(E) = K_E \cdot \Fix_{G'}(E)$, the orbit equality $gE = hF$ is equivalent to $\exists n \in G',\, nE = F \wedge g^{-1}hn \in K_E$.

  • IsQuasiFundDomain.qfd_equiv_hom — the Hom-set decomposition (lem:qfd_equiv): elements $g \in G$ with $g\cdot E \preceq E'$ factor uniquely as $g = n \cdot k$ with $n \in G'$ ($n\cdot E \preceq E'$) and $k \in K_E$. Derived from orbit_iff_K applied to $gE = 1 \cdot (gE)$ and taking the inverse.
