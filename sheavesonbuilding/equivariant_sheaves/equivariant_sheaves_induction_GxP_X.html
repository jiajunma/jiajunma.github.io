Induced space $G \times^P X'$ along a cosheaf $P$

Let $G' \le G$ act cellularly on $X'$, and let $P$ be a cosheaf on $X'$ of subgroups of $G$, $x \mapsto P_x$, satisfying:

  • (P1) $n P_x n^{-1} = P_{n \cdot x}$ for $n \in G'$;
  • (P2) $P_x \cap G' \subseteq \Fix_{G'}(x)$.

(P1) implies that $\Fix_{G'}(x)$ normalizes $P_x$.

Definition. The induced space $$X := G \times^P X' \;:=\; (G \times X') / \sim$$ where $$(g,x) \sim (h,y) \iff \exists n \in G' \text{ with } n \cdot x = y \text{ and } g^{-1}hn \in P_x.$$

The $G$-action is $g \cdot [(h,y)] = [(gh,y)]$ and $X$ carries the quotient topology.

Properties

  • Taking $P_x = \{e\}$ for all $x$ recovers the standard induced set $\mathrm{ind}_{G'}^G(X')$.
  • The construction depends on $P$ only up to the family $\Fix_{G'}(E) P_E$.
  • The map $\iota : X' \to X,\ x \mapsto [(1,x)]$ is injective.

After identifying $X'$ with $\iota(X')$, $X$ inherits a regular CW structure in which the $G$-action is cellular and $X'$ is a QFD for it (see CW structure and QFD property on G ×^P X' (Prop 2.8)).

Stabiliser formula (Sheaf7 lem:Stab_gx)

Lemma. For $g \in G$ and $x \in X'$: $$\Fix_G(g \cdot x) = {}^g\bigl(\Fix_{G'}(x) \cdot P_x\bigr), \qquad > P_E \subseteq \Fix_G(E) \quad\text{for every cell } E.$$ In particular $\hat K_x := \Fix_G(x) = \Fix_{G'}(x) \cdot P_x$ (for $x \in X' \subseteq X$ via $\iota$).

Proof

By $G$-equivariance ($\Fix_G(g \cdot x) = g \Fix_G(x) g^{-1}$), suffices to treat $g = 1$.

($\supseteq$). Take $\hat g = u \cdot p$ with $u \in \Fix_{G'}(x)$, $p \in P_x$. We show $[(up, x)]_\sim = [(1, x)]_\sim$. Apply the $\sim$-relation definition with $n := u \in G'$: $n \cdot x = u \cdot x = x$ (since $u \in \Fix_{G'}(x)$), and $(up)^{-1} \cdot 1 \cdot n = p^{-1} u^{-1} u = p^{-1} \in P_x$. Hence $\hat g$ fixes $x$ in $X$.

($\subseteq$). Take $\hat g \in \Fix_G(x)$. Then $\exists n \in G'$ with $n \cdot x = x$ (so $n \in \Fix_{G'}(x)$) and $\hat g^{-1} n \in P_x$, i.e.\ $\hat g \in n P_x^{-1} = n \cdot P_x$. By (P1), $\Fix_{G'}(x)$ normalises $P_x$, so $n P_x = P_x n \subseteq \Fix_{G'}(x) \cdot P_x$.

Second statement ($P_E \subseteq \Fix_G(E)$): for $p \in P_E$ and $x \in E$ relative interior, $P_E \subseteq P_x$ ($P$ cosheaf $\Rightarrow P_E = P_x$ on the open cell), so $p \in P_x \subseteq \Fix_G(x)$ by the first statement. Since this holds for every $x \in E$ rel int and $\Fix_G(E) = \bigcap_{x \in E} \Fix_G(x)$, $p \in \Fix_G(E)$. $\square$
