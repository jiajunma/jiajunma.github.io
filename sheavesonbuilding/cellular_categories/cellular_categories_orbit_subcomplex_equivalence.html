QFD inclusion $[X'] \hookrightarrow \,]X/G[\,$ is an equivalence

Following Sheaf7 line 725-728.

Let $S \subseteq X$ be a QFD for the subgroup $G' \le G$ (Quasi-fundamental domain). Denote by $[X']_{[X/G]}$ (equivalently $\,]X'[_{]X/G[}\,$) the full subcategory of $]X/G[$ on cells of $S$.

Proposition. The inclusion functor $$\iota : \,]X'[_{]X/G[}\; \longrightarrow\; \,]X/G[$$ is an equivalence of categories. Dually, $$\iota : [X']_{[X/G]}\; \longrightarrow\; [X/G]$$ is an equivalence.

Proof

A full-subcategory inclusion is automatically fully faithful. Essential surjectivity is exactly the QFD hypothesis: every cell $F \in X$ lies in the $G$-orbit of some cell of $S$ (Quasi-fundamental domain). Concretely, given $F \in X$, choose $g \in G$ and $E \in S$ with $g \cdot E = F$; then $g : E \to F$ and $g^{-1} : F \to E$ are mutually inverse morphisms in $]X/G[$ (both witnesses are $g E = F \preceq F$ resp.\ $g^{-1} F = E \preceq E$; composition is $\mathbf 1 = g \cdot g^{-1}$). $\square$

Lean formalization

The full subcategory is defined using mathlib's CategoryTheory.ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory:

abbrev OnSubcomplex (S : Subcomplex X) :=
  ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory (C := TransporterCat G X) (fun E => (E : X) ∈ S)

abbrev ιOnSubcomplex (S : Subcomplex X) : OnSubcomplex S ⥤ TransporterCat G X :=
  ObjectProperty.ι _

ιOnSubcomplex_essSurj provides the essential surjectivity, using IsQuasiFundDomain.surj to obtain the pair $(g, F)$ and constructing the iso as Iso.mk with .hom = ⟨g, _⟩ and .inv = ⟨g⁻¹, _⟩.

ιOnSubcomplex_isEquivalence then packages Functor.IsEquivalence (mathlib gives full + faithful for free from full-subcategory inclusions; this file supplies essential surjectivity). Stated as a theorem (not instance) because G' does not appear in the conclusion, so must be letI'd at call sites.
