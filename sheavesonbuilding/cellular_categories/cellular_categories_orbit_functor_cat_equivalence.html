$\underline{\mathrm{Fun}}([X']^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C)$

Corollary of QFD inclusion [X'] ↪ ]X/G[ is a categorical equivalence.

Corollary. If $S$ is a QFD for $G' \le G$, then for any category $\mathscr C$ $$\underline{\mathrm{Fun}}\bigl([X']^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C\bigr) \;\simeq\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}\bigl(\,]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}},\,\mathscr C\bigr).$$

Proof

Full-subcategory inclusion $\iota : [X'] \hookrightarrow \,]X/G[$ is an equivalence by QFD inclusion [X'] ↪ ]X/G[ is a categorical equivalence. Passing to opposite categories preserves equivalences, and the functor Equivalence.congrLeft sends an equivalence $\mathcal A \simeq \mathcal B$ to an equivalence $\underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(\mathcal A, \mathscr C) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(\mathcal B, \mathscr C)$. $\square$

Lean formalization

noncomputable def funCatEquivOfQFD (G' : Subgroup G) (S : Subcomplex X)
    [IsQuasiFundDomain G' S] :
    ((OnSubcomplex S)ᵒᵖ ⥤ C) ≌ ((OrbitCat G X)ᵒᵖ ⥤ C) :=
  letI := ιOnSubcomplex_isEquivalence G' S
  Equivalence.congrLeft (Functor.asEquivalence (ιOnSubcomplex S).op)

Uses Functor.op (preserves IsEquivalence) composed with mathlib's Equivalence.congrLeft. [X/G]) this gives the first equivalence $$\Cosh_G(X, C) \;\simeq\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}([X']^{\mathrm{op}}, C)$$ of Sheaf7 Prop 2.6.3 (Reduction of G-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD).
