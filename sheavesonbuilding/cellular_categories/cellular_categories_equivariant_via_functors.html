$G$-equivariant (co)sheaves as functors on $]X/G[$ resp.\ $[X/G]$

Given a cellular $G$-action on $X$ (Locally finite regular CW complex with cellular G-action), the two nominally independent structures — the poset (face relation) and the group action — are packaged together in the two transporter categories $]X/G[$ and $[X/G]$ (The transporter categories ]X/G[ and [X/G]). Every equivariant sheaf and cosheaf on $X$ arises as a functor on the appropriate one.

Statement

Proposition. For any category $\mathscr C$:

$$ \Sh_G(X, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(\, ]X/G[,\; \mathscr C\, ) \qquad\text{and}\qquad \Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(\, [X/G],\; \mathscr C\, ). $$

Equivalently, $\Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(\, ]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}},\; \mathscr C\, )$.

Taking $G = \{e\}$ recovers $\Sh(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}(]X[, \mathscr C)$ and $\Cosh(X, \mathscr C) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Fun}}([X], \mathscr C)$.

Sketch of the equivalence (sheaf case)

Given $(V, a) \in \Sh_G(X, \mathscr C)$, define $F :\, ]X/G[\, \to \mathscr C$ by

  • $F(E) := V_E$;
  • for $g \in \Hom_{]X/G[}(E, E')$ (i.e.\ $g \cdot E \preceq E'$), $F(g) := r^V_{g \cdot E,\, E'} \circ a^V_{g, E}$.

Functoriality ($F(h) \circ F(g) = F(hg)$ for composable $g : E \to E'$, $h : E' \to E''$) is a direct chase using the equivariance axiom of $a^V$ and transitivity of $r^V$.

Inverse. Given $F :\, ]X/G[\, \to \mathscr C$, set $V_E := F(E)$, $r^V_{E, E'} := F(e : E \to E')$ (the identity element $e \in G$ as a morphism $E \to E'$ whenever $E \preceq E'$), and $a^V_{g, E} := F(g : E \to g \cdot E)$.

Cosheaf case is dual: $F : [X/G] \to \mathscr C$ takes morphisms in the reverse direction, matching corestriction.

Corollary: stalk actions as endomorphism actions

Under the equivalence, the stalk action of $\Stab_G(E)$ on $V_E$ (a $G$-equivariant sheaf/cosheaf datum) is the endomorphism action $\End_{]X/G[}(E) \cong \Stab_G(E)$ on $F(E)$ (The transporter categories ]X/G[ and [X/G]).

mapOfG_mul's proof is a straightforward but tedious diagram chase using act_map (naturality) + act_mul (multiplicativity) + eqToHom bookkeeping. It is being filled in.

Architectural obstruction on toFunctor. The body of GCosheaf.toFunctor is written out but cannot be typechecked under the current TransporterCat-as-abbrev design. With

abbrev TransporterCat (G X : Type*) : Type _ := X

the type X carries two competing Category X instances:

  • the preorder-derived one (from [PartialOrder X]) — used by Cosheaf X C = Xᵒᵖ ⥤ C;
  • the scoped TransporterCat.category — used by (TransporterCat G X)ᵒᵖ ⥤ C.

When both meet in one term (which is exactly what toFunctor does), the elaborator's isDefEq search on the derived Category Xᵒᵖ loops. Bumping synthInstance.maxHeartbeats does not help — the two derivations produce non-defeq Hom types, so no attempt succeeds.

Fixes. Two clean options:

(a) refactor TransporterCat from abbrev to a structure wrapper, mirroring the design chosen for The category [X'/P] and its equivalence with the transporter category of G ×^P X''s PXPrimeTransporterCat — a genuinely fresh type carries only its own Category instance; (b) rework Cosheaf so its Xᵒᵖ no longer resolves through the ambient Category X.

Both are substantial refactors touching a lot of downstream declarations (TransporterCat → OnSubcomplex, ιOnSubcomplex, funCatEquivOfQFD, endMorEquivStabilizer, etc.); (a) is the cleaner and localises the change to TransporterCat.lean plus its direct users. Neither is done yet.

Sheaf side. Not started. Once the Cosheaf side lands and the architectural fix is in place, the Sheaf $F(g) = r \circ a$ construction is mechanically dual.

Consequences for the reduction

The reduction theorem Reduction of G-equivariant cosheaves to a QFD $$ \Cosh_G(X, \mathscr C) \;\simeq\; \Fun([S]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C) $$ is fully Lean-formalised on its categorical scaffolding half (funCatEquivOfQFD in SheafOnBuilding.TransporterFunEquiv) but still leans on the pending $\Cosh_G \simeq \Fun(]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}})$ piece for closure.

Similarly the induced-space form The category [X'/P] and its equivalence with the transporter category of G ×^P X': the equivalence $[X'/P] \simeq ]X/G[$ is fully Lean-formalised (as PXPrimeTransporterCat.toInducedTransporterCat_isEquivalence), so once the Cosh ≃ Fun forward direction lands, the reduction

$$ \Cosh_G(G \times^P X', \mathscr C) \simeq \Fun([X'/P]^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathscr C) $$

composes automatically via Equivalence.congrLeft on $\iota_*^{\mathrm{op}}$.
