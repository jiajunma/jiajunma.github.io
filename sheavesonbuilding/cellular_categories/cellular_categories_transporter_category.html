The transporter categories $\,]X/G[\,$ and $\,[X/G]\,$

Let $G$ act cellularly on the cell complex $X$ (Locally finite regular CW complex with cellular G-action). The $G$-action on the face poset $]X[$ produces two related categories $]X/G[$ and $[X/G]$ — the transporter categories in the sense of categorical / algebraic-$K$-theoretic usage (Quillen, Grayson). They are a small model of the (weak) quotient of $]X[$ by the $G$-action, i.e.\ of the quotient stack $[X/G]$ for the discrete $G$-action on the face poset.

Definition

Definition ($]X/G[$). The transporter category $]X/G[$ has:

  • objects: cells of $X$;
  • morphisms: $\Hom_{]X/G[}(E, E') := {g \in G : g \cdot E \preceq E'}$;
  • composition: $g_2 \circ g_1 := g_2 g_1$ (well-typed because $(g_2 g_1) \cdot E = g_2 \cdot (g_1 \cdot E) \preceq g_2 \cdot E' \preceq E''$ using cellularity of the $G$-action);
  • identity: $\mathbf 1 : E \to E$ from $\mathbf 1 \cdot E = E \preceq E$.

We write $g : E \to E'$ for the morphism corresponding to $g \in \Hom_{]X/G[}(E, E')$.

Definition ($[X/G]$). $[X/G] := \,]X/G[^{\mathrm{op}}$.

Inversion $g \mapsto g^{-1}$ on $G$ gives a bijection $\Hom_{]X/G[}(E, E') \cong \Hom_{[X/G]}(E', E)$, so equivalently $$ \Hom_{[X/G]}(E', E) = \{g \in G : E \preceq g \cdot E'\}. $$

Trivial-group specialisation. For $G = \{e\}$, $]X/\{e\}[ \,=\, ]X[$ and $[X/\{e\}] = [X]$ ([[node:cellular_categories.category_jXk]]).

Terminology remark

The name transporter category is standard in algebraic $K$-theory (Quillen, Grayson) for exactly this kind of construction — objects with a partial order (or more generally a category) carrying a group action, morphisms being group elements that "transport" one object to a face of another. Equivalent names in adjacent traditions:

  • Action category / translation category (categorical topology, e.g.\ Baez–Dolan);
  • Grothendieck construction $\int_G ]X[$ of the constant $G$-diagram with value $]X[$;
  • The small model of the quotient stack $[X/G]$ (stack language, for discrete $G$).

Sheaf7 §2.3 calls these "orbit categories". Warning: in equivariant homotopy theory (Bredon, tom Dieck, Elmendorf, $\dots$) the term orbit category is reserved for the category of coset $G$-sets $G/H$ with $G$-equivariant maps, which is a different construction. Throughout this KB we use "transporter category" for $]X/G[$, $[X/G]$ to avoid the clash. The Lean identifier SheafOnBuilding.TransporterCat is a naming holdover from the paper.

Endomorphisms are stabilisers

An endomorphism $g \in \Hom_{]X/G[}(E, E)$ has $g \cdot E \preceq E$; because the $G$-action preserves dimension (Cellular G-action on an abstract cell complex) and $\dim$ is strictly monotone on proper faces (Abstract cell complex), $g \cdot E = E$. Hence $$ \End_{]X/G[}(E) \;\cong\; \Stab_G(E) \qquad \text{as monoids}, $$ and dually $\End_{[X/G]}(E) \cong \Stab_G(E)$.

The rest — how functors on $]X/G[$ (resp.\ $[X/G]$) recover $G$-equivariant sheaves (resp.\ cosheaves), and how the above endomorphism identification realises the stalk actions — is G-equivariant (co)sheaves as functors on ]X/G[ resp. [X/G].

Lean formalization

Only $]X/G[$ carries a fresh Lean type; $[X/G]$ is (TransporterCat G X)ᵒᵖ.

structure TransporterCat (G X : Type*) : Type _ where
  cell : X
def Mor (E E' : TransporterCat G X) : Type _ :=
  {g : G // g • E.cell ≤ E'.cell}
instance category : Category (TransporterCat G X) where
  Hom := Mor
  id  := Mor.id
  comp := Mor.comp

Design points:

  • TransporterCat G X is a structure wrapping X with a single cell : X field. Genuinely fresh type, so its Category instance does not compete with X's Preorder-derived category — no scoped gymnastics needed.
  • Mor E E' is a Subtype of G (not a bespoke structure) so its universe matches G's, satisfying Category's constraints.
  • G is phantom on the type level, present only to key the Category instance by G.

(An earlier abbrev-based encoding created two competing Category X instances that collided in mixed contexts such as the forward-direction functor GCosheaf.toFunctor. The structure wrapper resolves this cleanly; see G-equivariant (co)sheaves as functors on ]X/G[ resp. [X/G] for the downstream packaging that this now unblocks.)

The endomorphism–stabiliser identification comes packaged three ways for downstream convenience:

def endMorEquivStabilizer (E : TransporterCat G X) :
    Mor E E ≃ MulAction.stabilizer G E.cell         -- Equiv
def endMulEquivStabilizer (E : TransporterCat G X) :
    End E ≃* MulAction.stabilizer G E.cell          -- MulEquiv
def endToStabilizer (E : TransporterCat G X) :
    End E →* MulAction.stabilizer G E.cell          -- MonoidHom

The ≃* upgrade needs care with CategoryTheory.End's convention $x * y = y \circ x$ (opposite of standard group multiplication); endMulEquivStabilizer handles that.
