Hypotheses (T1)–(T5) hold for classical groups

Lemma. For the Stevens construction, the cellular structure on $A$ given by hyperplanes $\{x_{i,l} \pm x_{i,l} = \tfrac{1}{2e}\}$ satisfies (F1)–(F4), and the data $(K_{x,+}, \phi_x)$ satisfies the type axioms (T1)–(T5).

Proof

of (T1)–(T4)

  • (T1)–(T4) follow from [KSS Prop 8.13] (using $S_1(\beta, \Lambda_x) = K_x \cap G_{x, 0+}$) together with [Schneider–Stuhler §II.2] (NOT BL Cor 4.5 — earlier KB drafts misattributed; BL Cor 4.5 is a lattice/ramification computation, unrelated) for the constancy on facets.

  • The unstated global existence step — that the local $\kappa_x$ assemble into a single $G$-equivariant family making (T1)/(T3) literally true (not just up to isomorphism at each $x$) — is the global $\beta$-extension compatibility, addressed in the Stevens-school literature as follows:

  • Local: [Stevens:SC §4.2] establishes existence at a single vertex; [Sko:SS Prop 7.10(ii)] (Skodlerack–Stevens) provides the refined intertwiner needed for compatibility.

  • Endo-parameter framework: [KSS21] formalizes the compatibility condition over a fixed maximal Levi via endo-parameters.
  • Global compatibility (formerly [HKSS Conj 8.14]): the Skodlerack–Ye preprint (Thm 5.21) closes the quaternionic case; see Global β-extension existence (compatible κ-family on B(G_β)).

The general classical case remains preprint-conditional pending publication of [SkoYe] and a documented extension of the quadratic-correction technique beyond the quaternionic setting.

Proof of (T5)

This is the geometric heart of the verification. Use the lattice-function language: $\Lambda_x$ and $\Lambda_y$ are also lattice functions for each $G^{(i)}$, so $z \in [x, y]$ in $\mathcal{B}(G^{(i)})$ for all $i$.

By [BS Lemma 3.1] (which gives the inclusion $G^{(i)}_{z, r} \subseteq G^{(i)}_{x, r} G^{(i)}_{y, r}$) plus the standard converse (each factor lies in $G^{(i)}_{z, r}$ since $z \in [x, y]$, so $G^{(i)}_{x, r} \cap G^{(i)}_{z, r}$ and similarly for $y$ are contained in $G^{(i)}_{z, r}$): $$G^{(i)}_{z, r} = (G^{(i)}_{z, r} \cap G^{(i)}_{x, r})(G^{(i)}_{z, r} \cap G^{(i)}_{y, r}) \qquad \forall r > 0,\ i = 0, \dots, d.$$

Decompose $K_{z, +, r} := K_{z,+} \cap G_{x, r}$ and induct on the jumps $r_1 < r_2 < \dots$ in $K_{z, +, \bullet}$. Setting $U^\pm_r := K_{z, +, r} \cap K_{x/y, +}$, the BS identity gives the seed $K_{z, +, r_k} = U^+_{r_k} U^-_{r_k}$ at the top level. The inductive step combines the BS factorization at level $r_{l-1}$ with a commutator manipulation: for $g \in K_{x, r_{l-1}}$, write $g = a_1 a_2 u_1 u_2$ and rearrange using $u_1^{-1} a_2 u_1 a_2^{-1} \in K_{z, +, r_l} = U^+_{r_l} U^-_{r_l}$ to obtain $g \in U^+_{r_{l-1}} U^-_{r_{l-1}}$. $\square$
