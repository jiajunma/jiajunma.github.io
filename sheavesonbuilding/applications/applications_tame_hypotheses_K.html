(K1)–(K3) and (F1)–(F4) hold for tame types

Proposition. With the Yu/AFMO data of [[node:applications.tame_K_F_construction]], hypotheses (K1)–(K3) of the generalized-building setup and (F1)–(F4) of the apartment cellular-structure setup hold.

Setup

The tame setting fixes a twisted Levi tower $G^{(0)} \subseteq G^{(1)} \subseteq \cdots \subseteq G^{(d)} = G$ over the depth vector $\vec r = (r_0, r_1, \dots, r_d)$ with $0 = r_0 < r_1 < \cdots < r_d$ (Tamely ramified setting: Yu / Kim–Yu / AFMO datum), an apartment $A = \mathcal A(T)$ of a maximal $F$-split torus $T \subseteq G^{(0)}$, and the Kim–Yu groups $J_x = \prod_{i=1}^d G^{(i)}_{x, s_i}$ for the half-shifts $s_i := r_i / 2$. The $K$-cosheaf stalk is $$K_x \;=\; G^{(0)}_{x, 0} \cdot J_x, \qquad K_{x,+} \;=\; G^{(0)}_{x, 0+} \cdot J_{x, +}, \qquad J_{x, +} \;:=\; \prod_{i=1}^d G^{(i)}_{x, s_i +},$$ and $\hat K_x = \Fix_G(x)$ is the $G$-fixator of $x$ when $A$ is embedded into $\BSigma = G \times^K A$ via $x \mapsto [e, x]$.

Proof

of (F1)–(F4)

The apartment $A$ is a finite-dimensional Euclidean polysimplicial complex equipped with the standard hyperplane arrangement of $T$ (root hyperplanes plus the shift hyperplanes $\alpha = m$ for the depths $s_1, \dots, s_d$). (F1) local finiteness and (F2) regularity are immediate. (F3) finite isotropy of the Weyl group action is standard. (F4) is checked stalk-by-stalk: the $G^{(i)}_{\bullet, s_i}$ are constant on facets of the chosen polysimplicial refinement by the Moy–Prasad construction.

Proof of (K1)

(K1) requires $K_x$ to be a compact open subgroup of $G$ for every $x \in A$, with $K_x = K_{x'}$ for $x, x'$ in the same open facet.

Compactness and openness of $K_x = G^{(0)}_{x, 0} J_x$ follow because each factor is compact open in its respective $G^{(i)}$, and the product (taken inside $G$) is closed and open by standard MP properties. Facet-constancy is by construction of $G^{(i)}_{\bullet, s_i}$ on the polysimplicial refinement (cf. (F4)).

Proof of (K2)

(K2) is the $N$-equivariance and the inclusion $G'x \subseteq K_x \cap G' \subseteq \Fix{G'}(x)$ where $G' = G^{(0)} = M$.

$N$-equivariance of $K$: for $n \in N \subseteq G^{(0)}$ and $x \in A$, ${}^n K_x = {}^n G^{(0)}{x, 0} \cdot {}^n J_x = G^{(0)}{nx, 0} \cdot \prod_i {}^n G^{(i)}_{x, s_i}$. Since $N$ normalizes each $G^{(i)}$ (twisted Levi structure) and $N$ acts on $A$ by isometries respecting the MP filtration, ${}^n G^{(i)}_{x, s_i} = G^{(i)}_{nx, s_i}$. Hence ${}^n K_x = K_{nx}$.

K-condition: - $G^{(0)}_{x, 0+} \subseteq K_x$ is immediate ($G^{(0)}{x, 0+} \subseteq G^{(0)}{x, 0}$). - $K_x \cap G^{(0)} = G^{(0)}_{x, 0} \cdot (J_x \cap G^{(0)})$. The intersection $J_x \cap G^{(0)} = \prod_i (G^{(i)}{x, s_i} \cap G^{(0)}) = \prod_i G^{(0)}{x, s_i}$ (twisted Levi factor intersection), so $K_x \cap G^{(0)} = G^{(0)}{x, 0} \cdot \prod_i G^{(0)}{x, s_i} = G^{(0)}{x, 0}$. This sits inside $\Fix{G^{(0)}}(x) = G^{(0)}{x, 0} \cdot \mathrm{stab}{N^{(0)}}(x)$, giving the second inclusion.

Proof of (K3) — the tame analogue

The classical-groups proof in [[node:applications.classical_K1_K2_K3]] used the enveloping-$\widetilde G$ trick (embed in $\mathrm{GL}_F(V)$, use lattice-function intersection); this is unavailable in the tame setting. We use the twisted-Levi tower directly.

Lemma (tame K3). For all $x, y \in A$ and all $z \in [x, y]$, $$\Fix_G(x) \cap \Fix_G(y) \;\subseteq\; \Fix_G(z).$$

Proof. $\Fix_G(x) = K_x \cdot \mathrm{stab}_N(x)$ where the stabilizer in $N \subseteq G^{(0)}$ contributes the translation part of the $T$-action fixing $x$. Since $\mathrm{stab}_N(x) \cap \mathrm{stab}_N(y) \subseteq \mathrm{stab}_N(z)$ trivially (point stabilizers in $N$ acting affinely on $A$ are convex-closed along geodesics), it suffices to show \begin{equation}\label{eq:K3-Kpart} K_x \cap K_y \;\subseteq\; K_z, \qquad z \in [x, y]. \end{equation}

Recall $K_x = G^{(0)}_{x, 0} J_x = G^{(0)}_{x, 0} \prod_{i=1}^d G^{(i)}_{x, s_i}$. The Iwahori-style factorization of $G^{(i)}_{x, s_i}$ along the twisted Levi tower (see [Yu, §15] for the original construction; [AFMO2 Lemma 3.6.12] for the modernized version; note: earlier KB drafts cited "Kim–Yu Cor 8.3" — that corollary does not exist; Kim–Yu §8 ends at Cor 8.2) gives $$K_x \;=\; \prod_{i=0}^d \bigl(G^{(i)}_{x, s_i} \cap G^{(i+1)}_{x, 0+}\bigr) \;\cdot\; G^{(0)}_{x, 0},$$ where we put $s_0 = 0$ and $G^{(d+1)} := G$ formally. Each factor is an MP-filtration piece of a single twisted Levi group, intersected multiplicatively.

Apply the standard MP-convexity-along-geodesics lemma in each $G^{(i)}$: by [Bruhat–Tits, Cor 6.4.46] (see also [BS, Lem 3.1] which gives the inclusion $G_{z,r} \subseteq G_{x,r}G_{y,r}$), \begin{equation}\label{eq:MP-convex} G^{(i)}{x, r} \cap G^{(i)}{y, r} \;\subseteq\; G^{(i)}_{z, r} \qquad \forall i,\ \forall r \geq 0,\ \forall z \in [x, y]. \end{equation}

Now take $g \in K_x \cap K_y$. By the iterated Iwahori factorization along the twisted Levi tower (Kim–Yu, ibid.), write $g$ uniquely as $g = g_0 g_1 \cdots g_d$ with $g_i \in G^{(i)}{x, s_i} \cap G^{(i+1)}{x, 0+}$ on the $x$-side. Computing the same factorization relative to $y$ and using uniqueness (Yu's lemma applies to both $x$ and $y$ inside $\mathcal B(G^{(d)})$, and $G^{(0)}_{x, 0}$ contains $T(\mathcal O_F)$), each $g_i$ lies in $G^{(i)}_{x, s_i} \cap G^{(i)}_{y, s_i}$. By \eqref{eq:MP-convex} applied at depth $s_i$, $g_i \in G^{(i)}_{z, s_i}$. Hence $g \in \prod_i G^{(i)}_{z, s_i} \cdot G^{(0)}_{z, 0} = K_z$.

This proves \eqref{eq:K3-Kpart}, and (K3) follows. $\square$

Comparison with the classical proof

The classical proof factored through the enveloping $\widetilde G = \mathrm{GL}_F(V)$ and used lattice-function intersections in $\widetilde{\mathfrak g}$. The tame proof avoids the enveloping algebra by working directly with the twisted-Levi factorization of $K_x$ and applying MP-convexity ([BS Lem 3.1] generalization) in each tower stratum. The two arguments give the same conclusion for groups in the intersection of both setups (e.g. $G = \mathrm{Sp}_{2n}$ with classical Stevens types vs. Kim–Yu tame types).

Alternative source: Dat–Fintzen

Both the Iwahori-style factorization ([AFMO2 Lemma 3.6.12], cf. Yu §15) and the MP-convexity statement \eqref{eq:MP-convex} have cleaner reformulations in [Dat–Fintzen 2026] (see the comparison table in yu_construction § Dat-Fintzen parallel):

  • The twisted-Levi factorization of $K_x$ corresponds to DF's decomposition along the wild-inertia filtration (DF §3.3).
  • MP-convexity along geodesics for each $G^{(i)}$ is restated explicitly in DF §2.3.

Reading the K3 proof through the DF lens makes the geometric content (MP convexity in each stratum) and the algebraic content (Iwahori factorization) cleanly separable.
