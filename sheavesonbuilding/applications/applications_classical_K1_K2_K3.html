lemma staged
#

Uses

  • Stevens groups K_x, K_{x,+} from a β-approximation sequence
  • Hypothesis (K3): Fix_G intersections lie in Fix_G along geodesics
  • K-cosheaf on the apartment with K-condition

Hypotheses (K1)–(K3) hold for classical groups

Lemma. With $K_x$, $K_{x,+}$ as in [[node:applications.classical_K_F_definition]], hypotheses (K1), (K2), (K3) of [[node:building.K_cosheaf]] and [[node:building.K3_hypothesis]] hold.

Proof
  • (K1) is immediate from the definition of $K_x$.
  • (K2) ($N$-equivariance of the K-cosheaf) follows from the standard $N$-equivariance of the Stevens groups $J^\circ$ and the $G'$-equivariance of the centralizer; see [Stevens:SC §3.4] for the relevant equivariance and [Stevens:SS §3.5] for the lattice-sequence framework. (Earlier drafts cited [Stevens:SC Lemma 2.6] — that lemma does not exist; §2 of Stevens:SC ends at [Stevens:SC Corollary 2.5].)
  • (K3) uses the enveloping general linear group trick. Let $\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}} := \mathrm{End}_F(V)$, $\widetilde G := \mathrm{GL}_F(V)$, $\widetilde G^{(i)} := \widetilde G^{\gamma_i}$. For each $x$ define the lattice function $\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}_{x, r} := {X : X \Lambda_x(t) \subset \Lambda_x(t+r)\ \forall t}$ and the centralizer pieces $\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}^{(i)}_{x, r}$.

Set $\widetilde{\mathfrak{j}}_x := \sum_{i=0}^d \widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}^{(i)}_{x, s_i}$. By [Stevens:SS §3.6 (Glauberman fixed-pt construction; $\hat K_x = \widetilde{\mathfrak j}_x \cap G$ implicit)], $\hat K_x = \widetilde{\mathfrak{j}}_x \cap G$. By induction on $d$ (BK 3.3.4 style), $$\widetilde{\mathfrak{j}}_x \cap \widetilde{\mathfrak{j}}_y = \sum_i (\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}^{(i)}_{x, s_i} \cap \widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}^{(i)}_{y, s_i}).$$

For $z$ on the geodesic of $x, y$ inside the building of $\widetilde G^{(i)}$, the standard root-group analysis gives $\widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}^{(i)}{x, r} \cap \widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}^{(i)}{y, r} \subseteq \widetilde{\mathfrak{g}}^{(i)}_{z, r}$. Summing and intersecting with $G$ gives (K3). $\square$
