$\kappa$-intertwiner is one-dimensional and $G^{(0)}$-trivial

Lemma. For $x, y \in \mathcal{B}^0$, $$\dim \Hom_{K_{y,0+} \cap K_{x,0+}}(\kappa_x, \kappa_y) = 1,$$ and $G^{(0)}_{y, 0+} \cap G^{(0)}_x$ acts on this Hom-space trivially.

Proof

This proof invokes several lemmas as black boxes — including Yu Prop 14.1 (global induction) and Yu Lem 12.4 / 12.5 / 13.6 / 13.7 (index calculus).

⚠️ Primary source is Fintzen 2021 + Dat–Fintzen 2026, not Yu 2001 directly. Yu's original proof of Prop 14.1 has the gap fixed in [Fintzen 2021]; [Dat–Fintzen 2026] §3.4 packages the corrected intertwiner calculation in the wild-inertia idempotent framework. See Fintzen 2021: correction to Yu's tame supercuspidal construction for the citation hierarchy. Yu 2001 is retained only for notation alignment.

Following the corrected Yu Prop 14.1 (as in Fintzen 2021 / DF §3.4), applied inductively via the two-term case Two-term intertwiner I(φ_y, φ_x) = G_{x,s} G' G_{y,s} (Yu Thm 9.4 generalized):

For the two-term case retain the notation of the intertwiner lemma; define the residue-field cosets $$V := J_x / J_{x, +}, \quad V^+ := (J_x \cap J_{y, +}) J_{x, +} / J_{x, +}, \quad V' := (J_x \cap J_y) J_{x, +},$$ and the symmetric $W$, $W^+$, $W'$.

[Yu Lemma 13.6] gives $|V| = |V^+| |V'|$ and $|W| = |W^+| |W'|$; [Yu Lemma 12.5] gives $|V'| = [J_x \cap J_y : (J_{x,+} \cap J_y)(J_x \cap J_{y, +})] / |W^+|$ and symmetrically for $|W'|$.

By [Yu Lemma 12.4], $$\dim \Hom_{J_y \cap J_x}(\kappa_x, \kappa_y) = \frac{[J_x : J_{x, +}]^{1/2} [J_y : J_{y, +}]^{1/2}}{[J_x \cap J_y : (J_{x,+} \cap J_y)(J_x \cap J_{y, +})]} = 1.$$

By [Yu Remark 12.7], $\Hom_{J_x \cap J_y}(\kappa_x, \kappa_y) = \Hom_{J_x \cap J_y}(\kappa_x[\phi|_{J_y \cap J_{x, +}}], \kappa_y[\phi|_{J_x \cap J_{y, +}}])$. By Heisenberg–Weil functoriality [Geradin (2.19)(2.20)], $G'_{x, 0+} \cap G_y$ acts trivially. $\square$

This is exactly (E3) of [[node:depth_zero_reduction.hypothesis_E]] for tame types.

Alternative source: Dat–Fintzen

The Yu lemmas 12.4, 12.5, 13.6, 13.7 used in the proof above are repackaged in [Dat–Fintzen 2026 §3.4] as a single lattice-index + Heisenberg–Weil dimension calculation. The DF version separates:

  • the geometric content (lattice indices for the intersection $J_x \cap J_y$) — DF §3.4 lattice argument;
  • the representation-theoretic content (Heisenberg–Weil functoriality) — DF §3.3 statement of Géradin (2.19)/(2.20).

For verifying the one-dimensionality and $G^{(0)}$-triviality of the $\kappa$-Hom in detail, the DF reformulation may be cleaner. See the mapping in [[node:references.yu_construction|yu_construction § Dat-Fintzen parallel]] for the explicit cross-references.
