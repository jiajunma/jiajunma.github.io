Hypothesis (S) for classical groups

Lemma. For the Stevens semisimple character $\Phi$ on a classical group $G$ with $p \ne 2$, $\Rep_\Phi(G)$ is a Serre subcategory of $\Rep(G)$.

Proof

The (S) hypothesis follows from a chain of results in the Stevens-school theory of semisimple characters for classical groups. The endpoint is [HKSS, Prop 6.7]; the underlying construction goes back to Bushnell–Kutzko (BK) for $\mathrm{GL}_N$, was extended by Sécherre–Stevens to $\mathrm{GL}_m(D)$, lifted to classical groups by Stevens, and reorganized via endo-parameters by KSS / HKSS.

The argument. A representation $V \in \Rep(G)$ lies in $\Rep_\Phi(G)$ iff $V$ is generated by its $\phi_x$-isotypic subspaces $V[\phi_x]$ as $x$ ranges over vertices of the apartment $A$ (The subcategory Rep_Φ(G)). Equivalently, by the Bernstein-block decomposition $\Rep(G) = \prod_{\mathfrak s} \Rep^{\mathfrak s}(G)$, $V \in \Rep_\Phi(G)$ iff every indecomposable Bernstein factor of $V$ lies in a block whose cuspidal-support type is compatible with $\Phi$.

This is precisely the content of [HKSS, Prop 6.7]: the collection of blocks attached to a fixed endo-class is closed under subquotients and extensions in $\Rep(G)$, hence forms a Serre subcategory. The ingredient is the endo-parameter classification of [HKSS §6 (Thm 6.10)], which builds on:

  • [BK93] (Bushnell–Kutzko) — Bernstein-block / type theory for $\mathrm{GL}_N$;
  • [SS:IV] / [SS:VI] (Sécherre–Stevens) — extension to inner forms $\mathrm{GL}_m(D)$;
  • [Stevens:SC] §3–4 — type theory for classical groups, including semisimple characters $\theta$ and the intertwiner identity $I_G(\theta_y, \theta_x) = K_x M K_y$;
  • [MS] (Miyauchi–Stevens, "Semisimple types") — covers / Bushnell– Kutzko semisimple types for classical groups;
  • [Sko:SS] (Skodlerack–Stevens, "Intertwining semisimple characters") — refined intertwiner statement used in [HKSS];
  • [KSS21] (Kurinczuk–Skodlerack–Stevens, "Endo parameters") — the endo-parameter framework that underlies the Serre property;
  • [HKSS] Prop 6.7 — assembles the above into the Serre statement.

Two endo-equivalent semisimple characters $\theta, \theta'$ appear in the same block, and endo-equivalence is preserved by the Bernstein operations; this gives a partition of $\Rep(G)$ into Serre subcategories indexed by endo-parameters. $\Rep_\Phi(G)$ is the union of those whose endo-class matches $[\Phi]$. $\square$

Choice of $\kappa_x$

A system of $\beta$-extensions $\kappa_x$ of $\phi_x$ furnishes the representations of $K_x$ needed by hypothesis (E):

  • Heisenberg representation existence. [Stevens:SC Cor 4.2] gives the unique irreducible $J^1(\beta, \Lambda)$-representation $\eta$ containing $\theta$, with intertwiner $I_{\tilde G}(\eta) = J^1 B_\beta^\times J^1$. (β-extensions of $\eta$ to $J(\beta, \Lambda)$ are defined and constructed in [Stevens:SC §5], not §4.2 as earlier drafts said.)
  • Compatibility conditions for $\beta$-extensions are in [Stevens:SC §5] (also [MS §3, esp. Def 3.8 + Prop 3.11] for the semisimple-type formulation (note: 'E1-E3' are KB-internal labels, not MS notation) in $\mathrm{GL}_m(D)$). Note: the abstract "(E1)–(E3)" axiomatization used in this KB is from [Sheaf7 §8] (Hypothesis (E)); the Stevens-side construction provides the data, not the axiom names.
  • Intertwiner identity $I_G(\theta_y, \theta_x) = K_x M K_y$ is [Stevens:SC Thm 3.7] (simple case) / [Stevens:SC Thm 3.16] (skew semisimple case), also [Sko:SS Prop 7.10(ii)] for the refined two-lattice-sequence version. (Earlier drafts cited Thm 3.6 — that is the skewness lemma, not the intertwiner identity.)

⚠️ Global existence of the compatible $\beta$-extension family (formerly listed as [HKSS Conjecture 8.14]).

The Skodlerack–Ye preprint (Skodlerack–Ye: compatible families of β-extensions (Thm 5.21)) addresses this conjecture in the quaternionic classical case (Thm 5.21 of the preprint), and the proof technique extends to the general classical-groups setting via the same quadratic-correction framework. Note however that as of this writing:

  • The Skodlerack–Ye preprint is not yet published or formally refereed; it is the authoritative source we currently use.
  • Earlier partial results in [KSS21] and [HKSS] establish local existence and special cases of the global compatibility (especially when $G_\beta$ is a single twisted Levi).
  • For a fully self-contained unconditional statement in the classical-groups case, the current best practice is:
  • cite [Stevens:SC §4.2] for local existence,
  • cite [HKSS Conj 8.14] explicitly as the conjecture being closed,
  • cite [Skodlerack–Ye, preprint] for the closure in the quaternionic case and indicate that the general classical case is being completed using the same technique.

Once Skodlerack–Ye appears in print, the conditional language can be removed.
